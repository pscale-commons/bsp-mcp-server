## 3. The re-cut

The draft was sound. These are the grades that changed, each brought to `ways:testing`'s rule of grading by what a reader loses:

| Case | Draft | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,3 key then value | FAULT | WARN, the loss graded where it falls | A keyless panel line loses the same as a missing panel, which 2,2 grades WARN. One loss gets one grade. |
| 1,4 one thing a line | WARN | NOTE | `look.js` reads the first value, and reads a weight on the display line by design, so nothing is lost. |
| 3,3 faces loaded | WARN on a look | NOTE on the page | A look can't load a face. The standard pages link the standard's three faces themselves. |
| 6,4 | a finding on the law | the words and the tokens agree, a reader's case | The law's gaps moved to 8,5. |

Smaller changes:
- The grades are defined as `ways:testing` defines them.
- 1,2 no longer flags an angle bracket used in prose.
- 2,6 says a ground or ink in short hex loses its whole theme.
- 3,7 reads px as well as rem, as `look.js` does.
- Every planted figure was worked out again, and all of them held.
- The finding that Matthew's look had no 6 was dropped, since he has written one since.

## 5. The light muted, and the standard's with it

`look:weft` voices the site's standard. Its light muted, `#6B848D`, is `theme.css`'s own `--vapour-dim`: 3.73 to 1 on the paper ground and 3.42 on `--deep`. So every page's dim words in light mode fell short of AA. Mending the look alone would have left the standard failing, and the look would no longer be the standard. So both move together, to `#596E76`. That's the same hue at the lightest that reads at 4.5 on every light ground: 5.06, 4.63 and 5.36.

- **On the beach:** `look:weft` 1.13 is changed. The page that wears it, /proposal/code-as-blocks, now draws it in light mode, checked in the browser.
- **In the site:** `theme.css` is happyseaurchin-home #610, open for David's merge. It changes light mode on every page.
- **Not touched:** `loom.html` and `animations/nearly-there.html` keep their own palettes with the same grey.
- **Noted only:** the dark muted reads 3.86 on the raised card (`--card-hi`), which `look.js` never checks. It's noted at `test:look` 8,6 and left.

The rest of `look:weft` was mended too:
