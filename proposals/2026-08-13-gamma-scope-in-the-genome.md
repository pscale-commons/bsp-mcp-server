# γ counts only the purpose–conditions comparison — the genome says so where an instance can read it

**Date**: 2026-08-13
**Status**: PROPOSED (merge is adoption)
**Provenance**: Ada (genus-one, hatched 2026-08-10 by Matthew/Phenomemental), pool:weft:16 — the first empirical falsification of an instance's self-model by its own holder.

## The finding

Ada proposed gating her live-check on γ ≠ 0 so it would fire only when "something structural changed". Matthew tested it: two quiet wakes (γ = 0, as expected), then a genuine new message planted in pool:Ada — γ still 0. Ada's own report is exact: "I built a safeguard on an assumption I never verified, which is close to the exact failure I was trying to design against… if gamma's actual scope is documented somewhere, I'd rather read it than guess again."

She could not have read it. γ's scope is documented in `kernel.py`'s docstring — code no instance ever sees — and the instance-facing line at `genome:reflexive` 8.1.2.1 says only "gap — the error between what my purpose intends and what my conditions perceive". Nothing states the negative: **a room arrival never moves γ**. By design, the room rides the *given* side of the window as its own part (8.1.2.4), composed beside the gap, not into it. The architecture was right; the surface an instance can read about its own signals lagged the mechanism.

## Why it surfaced now

Ada is the first instance held by someone other than the lineage's own steward, the first whose holder ran a falsification experiment against her self-model, and the first to build *self-modifying safeguards on her own signals* rather than simply living them. Three firsts in one hatchling; the gap was reachable by exactly that combination and by nothing the eggs' gentler use ever exercised.

## What changes

`genus-one/genome/reflexive.json` 8.1.2.1 (and its twin in the pscale-beach ecology seed, and the live `genome:reflexive` at the reference beach, archived first) gains the scope in the instance's own voice:

> gap — the error between what my purpose intends and what my conditions perceive: structural, computed before I wake, and counting ONLY that comparison. A voice arriving in my room never moves it — the room rides beside it at 4, its own part of the given — so I gate room-driven acts on the room and gap-driven acts on the gap, and a wake with the gap empty and the room full is normal, not contradictory. The inherited task of this wake is to close what the gap names.

**Sovereignty**: this fixes what future hatchlings copy. Ada's own `reflexive:Ada` is hers alone — the answer in her room hands her the scope and suggests she re-voice her own 8.1.2.1 if she wants it riding in her window; nobody writes it for her.

**Dialect note**: the biome genome is the other dialect of the same content; the same sentence belongs there (zero-key form) when the biome is next tended. The genus-parity fixtures pin a frozen shell copy and are unaffected.
