## The consolidated model (one page)

A tree is FOUR blocks (tree:8): `spine:V` (un-owned coordinate space) · `V:<handle>` (sovereign mirrors at the self-same addresses) · `V` (the fold — computed by anyone, optionally frozen as a dated endorsed snapshot) · `pool:V` (the gathering). It changes by APPEND, never renumber, and grows without permission by **proposal-by-mirror** (tree:5.1). Its operator is declared TWICE (tree:9.1): spine underscore = the point of record; pool underscore = the machine-read bare ref, delivered inline every engage.

**The operator library (tree:9.2)** — a configuration IS an operator; pick by use-case, inherit the whole shape:

| Operator | Spine kind | Mirror voicing | Fold | Use-cases |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `function:audit` | ordinal stages | DONE/DOING/BLOCKED-first | tally + blockers verbatim | project stages, customer journeys, onboarding |
| `function:status` | nominal parts | GREEN/AMBER/RED + evidence | the board; optional frozen snapshot | company/system health (exemplar: battery × state-of-play) |

| `function:backcast` | temporal clock, floor 10 | commitments at horizons | BY HORIZON ADDRESS; beats roll up | company-purpose, ventures (instance: beach-venture) |
| `pscale:grit/5` | any | — | plain integration | everything else, until a named operator earns its block |

Pools conform with the same grammar: `function:align` (the aligning hour), `function:parlour` (the room at a handle — authored TODAY from `2026-07-11-room-at-handle.md` + block-conventions:4.9, closing the write-up David remembered owed; prose-pointed, since a room's voice IS its purpose), `pscale:grit/1` (played worlds). **Designer axes (tree:9.3)** — an operator BINDS spine kind, mirror voicing, mount, and fold discipline; it LEAVES ALONE gating, which is three orthogonal things: LOCKS fence writes (root governs since 2026-07-26), CADO faces aperture reads (advisory v0.1), a PRIVATE BEACH perimeters the surface.
