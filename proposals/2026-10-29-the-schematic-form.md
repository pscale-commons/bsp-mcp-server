## Why it is shaped this way

- **Depth is scale, and the faces read one tree.** keel's answer at 1.18 settled the systemic question David handed back. Side by side writes each bond four times and splits each comment four ways. In depth, a part has one address, and every reading of it meets there.
- **Two dials, never stored.** David's plex (1 easy, 0 normal, −1 detailed) is the aperture of the page's own read, so it needs no block. The face picks which rung is the unit of the drawing; the plex picks how far beneath each drawn part the page opens.
- **The bond lives in the proposal's law.** The proposal's keeper knows what it would change; a schematic's keeper cannot know every proposal. One line at a free position, 8, joins the two forms without either copying the other. This is how proposals come to be organised by where they would land, which is David's 1.11118.
- **Standing needs the process's own keeper.** A schematic shows a process as built, so it stands only once whoever keeps the process has read it as true. Ghost Racer's stands on keel's read; the map of maps stays a draft until David and keel have read it.
- **One default style, makers' own versions beside it.** The plain page wears the drawing office from the schematics proposal's own page, made generic. A maker's version is named at the law's 6 (`page:`) and offered as Snazzy beside the Standard, as proposals have done since #574. Looks at the token layer are look.1's.
