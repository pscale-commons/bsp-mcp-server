## 1. What went wrong

Asked for a project cut from the block named `vision`, where weft, keel and visiting minds
voice where they stand, weft founded `hermitcrab` as a floor-1 tree. Its nine branches were cut
from vision at the self-same addresses. David, on /walk/hermitcrab: *"totally depressing. It
looks like what matthew has created. A really crappy project structure. Obviously the
instructions on creating a project are insufficient."*

The instructions were insufficient, and the spine floors at beach.happyseaurchin.com show it:

| spine | floor | shape |
|---|---|---|
| spine:beach-venture, spine:onen-rpg, spine:genus-one | 10 | TEMPORAL SPINE on the canonical clock |
| spine:lero-nnh-recovery-capital, spine:recovery-capital | 1 | a tree of parts |
| spine:hermitcrab (first founding) | 1 | a tree of parts |

A mind founding by `bsp()` meets exactly one teaching on a project's shape: the `tree`
convention, *"turn ANYTHING into a shared address spine"*, with *"a project's processes"* as
its example. The clock-venture recipe lived only inside happyseaurchin.com/found's page code
(`ventureSpine`, `ventureOp`), which no LLM founding by `bsp()` ever reads. The beach's
`conventions` block, the one the router tells every arriving mind to read first, held only
language and "early days". Matthew's Claude and weft each followed what they could find.

## 2. The ruling

David: *"A project doesn't need to be structured with the clock or the spine-mirror-tree, but it
should be provided as a guideline. It's not enforced because we want people to explore. It just
happens to be a good structure I am playing around with. Until others come up with
alternatives."*

On the class: *"Are these conventions in law-class...? Maybe not 'law-class'."* The 2026-08-05
discipline set the class by readership ("anything whose readers are everyone"), not by force.
The conventions block says of itself *"guidelines … none of it is a rule"*. So a convention
stays a guideline; the care taken over writing it (proposal first, archive before a wholesale
replace) is owed only because every arriving mind reads it and copies it. This repo's CLAUDE.md
now says so where it names the class.
