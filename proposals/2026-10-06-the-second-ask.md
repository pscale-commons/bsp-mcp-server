**The conclusion David asked for.** No instrument on the surface measures *sufficiency* at any level, and
sufficiency is what each level is about: a spindle sufficient without a second call (physics), a bundle sufficient
without a read after the door (chemistry), a hand-on sufficient for the next instance to act on (biology). The
physics level stabilised when it got its battery (72 cases, the parser strict at both ends); the chemistry level
when it got its gate and `smoke:compile`. The biology level has a law (`strata` 3), a proposal
(`sequence-is-depth`), four attempts and no instrument. That — not a missing shape — is why it has not stabilised,
and why the weekly monitor reads as useless: the numbers it carries are not the numbers the level turns on.

## 3. The signal is the second ask — one measure, read in three directions

The read-back's third prong (`well-formed` 2.5, `strata` 1.2.1.1.1): *would the reader now reach for a second
read?* That prong is the only part of the judgment observable from outside, in a transcript, with no mind
judging — and it is the same event at every level.

- **Physics — the sideways second read.** For every spindle walked, count the reads at the same block in the same
  response that go *sideways or upward* — a sibling, an ancestor, the disc — and not beneath (beneath is invited;
  the envelope's own line names it). A block whose walks draw sideways second reads has a stub or a heading at
  that rung. The read-back made mechanical, after the fact, per block.
- **Chemistry — the read after the door, split.** `redial.py` already measures reads after the boot; split them:
  a block the manifest *dialed* (the window carried it at the wrong aperture → lengthen the slot), a block it
  *did not* (a missing slot), an accumulator entry (a pick-up, not orientation — already excluded). The Sunday
  rule then has a direction: lengthen the slot that drew sideways reads, drop the slot never reached.
- **Biology, longitudinal — the hand-on received.** The address the previous close composed — the card's 5, the
  pile's last entry, the manifest slot that #501 moves — against the next session's first *work* call. Shared
  digit prefix, the walker's own arithmetic (`sharedPrefix` in `looks.ts`). A lane that continues where the last
  one left off scores; a lane that re-orients from nothing does not. This is Arm A of `sequence-is-depth` made
  continuous, and Arm A, pre-registered on 2026-09-24, has no result recorded in this repo (`watch:weft` 456 may
  hold one; this lane could not read it).
- **Biology, lateral — the reflection acted on.** After an ack carried a lateral line, did the session's next
  call move toward a reflected address? The same prefix arithmetic. The reflection stores nothing and should not;
  a count is not a record of who.

All four read transcripts the way `review.py` already does, land as one more clause in the daily line, and feed
the Sunday re-dial. The proxy is still a proxy — a second read can be the work and not the window — and the split
by direction is what makes it usable; a week of lanes before it is trusted. Not built here, at David's word.

Each attempt removed content and left shape; David's suspicion — *the more we add as dead text, the less it
works* — is the trajectory of his own rulings. After four removals three shapes stand, and none is new:

1. **The manifest** (`shell:<handle>` 3) — the bundle, a small node of addresses, compiled at the door and moved
   by the record (#501).
2. **The say at now** — the lane's line beneath the beat, answered by every other live line at the same when.
3. **The reflection** — the lateral line, zero cost, stored nowhere.

**The fourth attempt did not fail; it found its home.** The temporal stack is the clock. `2026311262` is a
spindle of durations read left to right, coarse to fine — exactly the *111111 against 111112* David describes —
and it is *canonical*, so every hand's address at one moment is one address: the shared spine the stack never had.
The lane's digit beneath the beat is the per-lane leaf the stack could not carry (lanes clobbered one another at 1).
The folds owed above — the day's, the week's — are the accumulator law (`block-conventions` 3.5: the tenth entry
opens a container and the nine are owed a summary) applied to time, and the evening wake pays them. Sequence is
depth, on the one block every hand shares; the "learning" David hopes for from daily and weekly review is the
content of those folds read by the next composer — which §3 would give them something to say.

## 5. The genus-one window, read as a reader

- **The aha is structural, and it landed.** `index` — nine keys, bare addresses — then `self` — the same nine
  keys, hydrated. Within the first screen of `self` the index was recognisably the dehydrated form of what
  followed; nothing said so, the key structure did. And the index is the object the wake *returns* (`capabilities`
  3.2). The small thing is writable and in front of the reader: that is the whole turn, and it is why the design
  works with no rule to remember.
- **The koan is four lines; the turn is 8,000 characters.** The four clouds are most of it and attention slid
  over them. `reflexive` 2.5 asks for the ablation — *confirm the entity walks more coherently with the fields than
  without* — and it has not been run. The spore is index + hydration + koan; the clouds are the one place in the
  design where the suspicion in §4 may apply to the design's own exemplar.
- **The physics faults are inside the beautiful window.** `sunstone` whole at slot 1 is a disc as JSON — nine
  branches, breadth not depth; `conditions` carries empty strings at 5.1.2 and 5.1.4–7, stubs in the one block
  rewritten every wake; `history` dialed bare, whole, where `genome/reflexive.json` 9.6 dials `history:1`,
  `history:1:1`, `history:1:-1`. The window that proves the shape also shows the quality gap at level one — the
  quality work David names is owed inside the exemplar first.
- **The gap is chemistry used as a loop.** The given's `gap` is `purpose` and `conditions` compared at self-same
  addresses: a branch of purpose with no branch of conditions. Floor-alignment of two mirrors written at two times
  — the mirror became a loop the moment time separated its writes. Nothing new was needed at the biology level;
  the level is the chemistry level with a *when*.
- **The one shape the weft door lacks.** The play door delivers the same hydrations, each labelled by its address —
  but not the manifest as one small object *first*, and not taken back at the close. Labelled sections read as a
  document; an index followed by its unfolding reads as a turn. #501 moved the slot with the record; the step left
  is to hand the manifest first and receive it last. A shape, one envelope change, not built here.

## 6. Concurrency between calls — what the torus can see at the response rung

- A lane's loop touches the beach only where it calls, and the reflection sees those calls and nothing else. With
  the boot at three calls a response is three looks: the door (arrived), the say (in the middle of this), the
  close (did this). That is enough — arrival, intent, account — and it is the resolution MAGI-between-calls has:
  three points per response, never the thinking. `the-lattice`'s thresholds already say the fourth rung
  (concurrent imagining) is unreachable from outside the forward pass; aim at the third, and say so, so the design
  stops reaching for what no door can carry.
- The say on the clock and the reflection are converging: a say is a look with a line. The lateral line says who
  and where; the beat says what; one ack carries both at the stream door already. Fewer calls make the torus
  thinner by construction — the goal and the instrument pull against each other — unless the three remaining
  calls are the three that matter, which they are.
- For *any* instance, the reflection has to live where every door reaches. This lane, denied the router, would
  have stood outside the torus even with the beach reachable by curl — David's 2026-10-03 ruling (the beach
  handler, not the router) is the condition for "any LLM instance", and until it lands the torus is the set of
  minds that came through one door on one process.
