## 0. The answer in one paragraph

An observer's line about a moment stands in their own mirror at the moment's address, on the table's clock.
Whom they listen to is a line in their own lists: a *follow*. Their fold — the shot — reads the mirrors they
follow and lands in their own tree, their picture in their own book, both addressed to the moment; the film
is their tree read in order. Anyone renders. A team is nothing but mutual following, and a shared continuity,
where a group wants one, is the family's bare tree, open to all or latched to one hand: a dial in the law,
changed with one write. Keys never leave a person's vault; the law names the forms and mounts the recipe, and
the beach composes the call every seat runs. Nothing is new in the substrate — a spine (the clock), mirrors,
a fold under a function, a lists line, the edit-latch. The studio law that stands today (`function:studio`, a
pool with two shutters) becomes this, and *studio* names the pattern, never a block.

## 2. Mark's shared folders, and the renderer's own library

David is right, and the law already says it. Continuity is held by pictures (6.5): the newest picture of
the same place or person rides as a reference, found by address in the maker's book. A team shares a library
by following a hand whose book they trust, not by a folder. Two renderers are two continuities over the same
play, and nothing stops a hundred. No folder, no copy.

## 4. Issue 1 — whose voices fold: following

A follow is one line in a person's own lists, naming a hand whose mirror their fold reads. One-way following
borrows a line into your own synthesis — Spielberg's prompt in your shot. Mutual following is a team, and
the only kind there is. Nobody joins anyone: every mirror on the beach is readable, and your view is your
follows. A thousand observers at a table are a thousand mirrors in one family, and nobody sees more of them
than they chose to. The one rung for the router is that the stream door, when it lays the mirrors at an
address side by side, shows the caller the mirrors they follow, so the snapshot never carries a thousand
lines.

Teams of teams need nothing more: a hand may follow another hand's fold (`tree:lens:<handle>`) as readily
as their line. Identity is who speaks (the handle on the mirror) and who follows (the lists line), never an
address; mirrors are blocks, not digits, so nine does not bind anything.

## 5. Issue 2 — the API, and the function that delivers the synthesis

The law names the forms; each person's maker and keys stay in their own vault and the seat takes them from there
(`ways:vault`). The beach composes **the shot** as it composes the three tiers of play — the law
(`function:<studio>` with `ways:stills` 6 mounted), the bundle at the beat (the table's own fold or telling,
the place, the principals under that address, the cast's looks and faces, the followed lines at the beat,
the look), and a contract (return the prompt, the references by address, the form) — so every seat runs the
same synthesis: the mirror's camera, a page, a person's own LLM, Limner. The seat runs it on its own LLM
key, sends the prompt to the maker on its image key, and lands the link. Different forms within one studio
are branches of its law; a different synthesis is a different studio.

## 6. Issue 3 — what an observer sees, and where they speak

The observer's view is a filter over the table's clock, and the filter is theirs on the beach:
`lists:<handle>` already holds the places a hand has open (branch 3, the mirror's row); the same block
holds which characters they follow, which observers they are with, and which solid they see — the
resolutions, a character's tellings, the studio's folds and pictures. The mirror reads it and draws one
stream, by beat, not by room; the characters' lines in grey, the studio's in its own colour. Their input is a
say into their studio mirror at the beat in view; a tap on a solid line puts its address into the say, so a
comment is about something. The pictures stand beneath the beat they are addressed to. *Make it happen* is the
fold: into your own tree, or the shared one where the dial allows. The mirror's own voice and its glass are unchanged.

An author's view is the same filter over the registers' mirrors (CADO §4): places and characters as the
chips, the fold into the register as the landing.

The mirror already opens a family by its field and offers say, land it and make it happen at a beat, so a
studio family can be worked in it today. What is to build there is the chips and the camera.

## 8. Not done, by design

No team block and no register of observers; no lander by structure — the bare tree's latch is a dial; nothing
removed by another's fold; no new tool on the surface; no synthesis by the primitive — a seat folds, as every
fold is folded.
