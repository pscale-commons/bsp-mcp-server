## 2. Three corrections to the 09-14 text

### 2.1 The determiner is the lock, and the fallback goes

§5 gets one night "from a role rather than the store", with a fallback — absent a keeper,
the party's earliest hand folds. That fallback is `log:urb-hitl` 5's rival-nights failure
by another name, and "by role" is store-enforced only when the night lives under ONE key.
So: the night at a beat is the keeper's kept fold, under the keeper's key, and nothing
else is a night. Absent the keeper, lines wait; a beat unfolded has not happened yet.
Supersession stays the keeper's alone and says what it supersedes.

This makes the honest shape of B visible: **the table dissolves as a URL and returns as a
key.** A party's keeper-key is its table. That is no loss — it is what lets a thousand
parties share one coordinate space at no central cost, because each party's keeper is one
of its own players' doormen wearing the seat on that player's fuel. A keeper the world's
host pays for would fail the substrate's own test (who pays at scale?).

### 2.2 The trial does not run at urb.beach

§7 recommends arm S at urb.beach "with personal keeps only". But S needs two law-class
blocks to exist where it runs — the ladder declaration and the operator — and the 08-06
law seals THREE flows: blocks, code and **vocabulary**. An operator written into David's
world is live instruction every later composer reads, whatever the verdict. So arm S
runs at an unlisted trial surface (a `/w/` name nothing links to), which reads the
scenario by reference exactly as a reference table does and holds only: the clock spine,
the operator, the seats' passports and mirrors, the keeper's keeps. Jettison is deleting
that surface; urb.beach is never touched. The ladder enters `rules:urb` only at rung 3,
if the trial earns it.

### 2.3 The family's names, with the tool as it stands

`pscale_stream_engage` composes over `spine:<field>`, `<field>:<handle>`,
`tree:<field>:<handle>` and `function:<field>`. With `field='temporal'` the mirrors
(`temporal:<handle>`) and keeps (`tree:temporal:<keeper>`) are exactly the 09-14 names;
only the spine (`spine:temporal`, a thin clock spine carrying the ladder and pointing at
`temporal:urb` for the epochs) and the operator (`function:temporal`) differ from
"`temporal:urb`" and "`function:urb`". No code. The named rungs ('today', 'now') compute
from the wall clock and are not used: in a played world a seat passes the beat address
its passport carries.

   leaving urb.beach untouched?* Recommended: yes (§2.2) — this reverses the 09-14
