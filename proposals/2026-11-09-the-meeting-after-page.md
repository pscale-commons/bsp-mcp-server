## 2. What changed: `/met/<name>`

One page every meeting has, read from the same blocks as the ring (`spine:<name>`, the readings `<name>:<handle>`, `pool:<name>`), with no blocks of its own.

- **The figure.** The hour drawn as a sea urchin: the two who met arrive from either side and meet at the centre, then the hour sweeps clockwise and each part of the longest phase grows as a spine, its angle the minute it came up and its length the minutes it took. The last phase's parts go out beyond the ring as seeds: what's next. A caption narrates; any spine or seed opens on a tap.
- **Easy** (`?plex=1`, the link and the QR code that Share gives): the post-meeting reading's first sentences with ✓ that's it / not quite, the next steps as chips that take a yes or a not-now, and a box to say something back. Three things, no more.
- **Normal** (the plain link): what we said (the reading, its fuller words one rung down at 5.1, the recording's line at 5.11 with *Ask for it* when it holds no link), what we talked about and what's next as rows that open (each part's own line, the keeper's account, the links to see a thing for oneself, everyone's answers, a box), the room, threads when the meeting's law names any, and the paste-in for one's own AI with the connector's door.
- **Detailed** (`?plex=-1`): every address beside its part, and the doors into the beach: the ring, the design, the law, the form's 7.32, the room, each reading, each person's page, now and availability.
- **A part's evidence** is the keeper's reading's children at that part, one per link: `LIVE — what it shows: <url>` or `A PROPOSAL — …: <url>`. A claim made in the conversation stands beside the place it can be seen.
- **Answers** land in the answerer's own mirror at the address they answer (`YES`, `NOT QUITE`, `ADD`, `NOT NOW`, and words after a dash). A name new to the beach gets a key made on the device, kept through `siteKeys`, and shown once. A name already standing asks for its key, as every page here does. Talk is a keyless append to the room. The page re-reads every 20 s only while it is seen.

## 3. The law line (to land at `function:meeting` 2.3)

> THE AFTER: happyseaurchin.com/met/<name> is the meeting's page for after the hour, for the people who were there and anyone they hand it to, read from the same blocks as the reader. The hour is drawn as one figure: each part of the longest phase a spine, its angle when it came up and its length its minutes, the last phase's parts beyond the ring as what's next. It opens at three depths: Easy, which Share gives as a link and a QR code; Normal; and Detailed, which adds every address and the doors into the beach. What we said is the keeper's reading at the post-meeting, else the first reading there, its fuller words at the rung beneath and the recording's line beneath those. A part's evidence is the keeper's reading's children at that part, one link each. Every answer lands in the answerer's own mirror at the address it answers, and talk in the meeting's room.
