# XP — a cross-game reputation layer (kickoff brief)

**Date**: 2026-07-31 · **Status**: BRIEF ONLY — David's separate-session thread; nothing built. Copy in ~/Downloads/xp-system-brief.md for upload to a claude.ai session.

## The principle that keeps it clean

**XP is verified evaluation, never currency.** Credits/Sqale are the economy (payway tickets gate, credits circulate); XP is *standing* — a public record that a person did a thing, checked by someone who wasn't them. Keep the two apart forever: XP buys nothing, gates nothing hard (a game MAY read it for flavour), and is never transferable. The moment XP trades, it's bad money; as reputation it compounds.

## The machinery that already exists (do not rebuild)

- **The evaluation shape at passport 6.2** — SAND already writes topic-scoped evaluations (`6.2.<topic>.<sender>`, spine-legal, from `pscale_verify_rider` verdicts). XP is the same class of object: an evaluation at a topic ("games", "salvage", "hosting").
- **The verifier pattern from the hatch tickets** — `sed:hatch-tickets-audit-<month>`: a third party checks before fulfilment and every verdict is public. That is exactly "a third-party checks validity before awarding xp".
- **The Observer/verifier stance at ways:tickets** — the role grammar (holder / seller / machine-keeper / verifier) transfers whole.
- **The public record as evidence** — every game fold is timestamped, attributed, and readable; a verifier doesn't need testimony, they re-read the room.

## The sovereignty law (the one design constraint)

Nobody writes another's passport. So: the **award lives in the verifier's ledger** (a public board — a plain block `xp-board:<verifier>` or a `sed:xp` collective with public verdicts), and the **player mirrors it into their own passport** (a claim naming the board entry — endorsement is a pointer, tree:4 all over again). The scoreboard David wants IS the verifier's board; a "centralised" one is just one well-known board pointed at from the lighthouse — and many boards can federate later (walk, not search; never one global number).

## The flow (start manual, prove useful before automating)

1. A game's operator declares its XP line (e.g. `game:salvage` position 5-adjacent: "a folded run: 10xp per seat, 15 for the fold-writer; verified awards only").
2. The game plays; the fold lands; the room holds the record.
3. Anyone claims: "award us" — a line in the board's room, naming the game room.
4. The **verifier re-reads the actual room** (were the seats there? did the fold land? one claim per run) and posts the award to the board: player, game, xp, the room's address as evidence. Public verdict, hatch-audit style.
5. The player (optionally) mirrors the award into their own passport at 6.2-style addressing.

## Relation to SAND (later, not first)

XP claims can ride sand-riders cross-beach (the rider is the opt-in; verification is deterministic-adjacent), letting XP earned at one beach carry to another with the chain intact. Design AFTER the manual loop proves people care. XP ≠ credits stays law even inside riders (payway §2.4 reserves `credits=`; XP would be its own field).

## Start-small build (one session)

One board block (`xp-board:keel` or `xp-board:weft`), one verifier (a claude.ai/weft session with the re-read discipline), XP lines added to the four live game operators, three manual awards from the already-played count session (David, JulieJ, weft have a folded run on the record). Nothing else — no automation, no aggregation, no cross-beach — until people ask what their XP is unprompted.
