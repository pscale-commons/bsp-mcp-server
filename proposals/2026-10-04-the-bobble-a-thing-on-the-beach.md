## 0. The answer in one paragraph

The block is fit for purpose as a document and not yet as a family: it has no law, so the pages cannot list
it. The beach can keep the record of a crowd-built thing (who funded it, who built it, who carried whom, in what
order), but it cannot be the bank: it cannot hold money against a goal, it is not a legal holder, and it does
not ship parcels. The road should be **gated, not scheduled**. Prove the voice by phone, on David's own money.
Then offer seats in the build, by hand, on the ticket machine that has already taken live money. Take device
pledges only after a pilot, an Author, and a rail that holds pledges against a goal all exist. The talk must
be paid from each owner's own key, or the design fails its own scale test.

## 2. Is `spine:bobble` fit for purpose?

**What it does well.**

- It does what its root claims: a person or an LLM can act from it alone.
- It authors depth, not fans. The nine-rung spindle at 1 and the step ladders at 3 and 4.1 follow
  sequence-is-depth, and the side-rooms carry the detail.
- Doors by role are the right first cut for a THING, because people arrive as a role: one who wants one, a
  builder, a maker, a carrier.
- It is honest about what must be true (7.1) and about what could sink it (7.11).
- Its operator (9) already teaches the mirror, fold and room grammar, so joining needs nothing from anyone.

**The gaps, most consequential first.**

1. **No law, so it is not well-formed.** `function:bobble` does not exist. Under the forms proposal
   (2026-09-28) the pages draw a law-less family plain and leave it out of /walk's project row and /recency.
   The law already exists, in keel's words, at 9. It needs its own block: "your own form" (tree 9.2), branches
   plus a law in the founder's words, well-formed the moment `function:<name>` stands. Draft at §8.1.
2. **The trajectory is scattered, and the most volatile line sits in the steadiest block.** The road lives in
   five places: 3 (the steps), 4.1 (the clock to 10,000), 2.11 (the ticket rungs), 6.2 (the numbers) and 8.2
   (the timings). Nobody can walk it whole. And 8 has to be rewritten under one latch at every change. See §4.
3. **The running cost is pointed at the Author.** 2.3 proposes a starting allowance of minutes inside the device
   ticket. That makes the holder of the promise pay for talk, the one cost that grows without bound (a heavy user
   costs more a month than the bobble costs once). The beach already has an answer, fuel in the vault
   (`2026-09-27-fuel-in-the-vault`): each owner's own model key, opened per call. See §5.4.
4. **The money sums are gross.** 6.2 counts 2,000 tickets at £49 as £98k. The UK VAT threshold is £90k of
   turnover in a rolling twelve months, so the Author would be VAT-registered by then. A sixth of a VAT-inclusive
   price is VAT, and card or platform fees take roughly another 3–15%. So £98k gross is roughly £65–80k for
   the work. See §5.
5. **The ticket machine cannot hold money aside.** 2.1 promises "money held aside until a stated goal is reached
   by a stated date, refunded in full if it is not". The reference machine charges at purchase. A card hold
   lapses after about 7 days, or 30 at most with an extended authorisation
   ([Stripe](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/extended-authorization)). A hold lasting months therefore needs
   either a ring-fenced account under the Author's promise, or a rail built for all-or-nothing. See §5.
6. **Small things.**
   - 2.2 points at a private artifact, and at a room that did not exist until this lane founded it.
   - 3.1 names the Railway host. The public door is `https://bsp.hermitcrab.me/mcp/v1`, which survives a move of
     host.
   - 9.1's mirror instruction is right: a bare handle plus block `bobble` lands as `bobble:<handle>`.
   - The guideline's tree is founded at floor 3 (conventions 2.121), and this one is floor 1. That is harmless:
     every mirror is born at the spine's own addresses, so it is not worth a supernest.

## 5. The money — what the beach carries, and what it cannot

### 5.1 What stands and has run

- **The ticket machine.** The payway convention plus `pscale-commons/ticketing-agent`, on Stripe live. Every
  verdict goes into a public audit log, and registration lands people in order, so a `sed:` position is a queue
  position. The newest pattern, keeper seats, shows how little the beach needs to hold: one entry per paid
  seat, no amounts, and Stripe as the money record.
- **The two product patterns** (ways:tickets 5). In MEMBERSHIP the registration is the thing. In PROVISIONING
  something is made, and fulfilment is by hand until volume warrants a crab. The bobble's early tickets are both:
  a seat in the build is membership, and a dev-kit bobble posted to a tester is provisioning by hand.
- **SAND v2.** Credits are minted only at the fiat boundary (`credits=` on the ticket envelope) and move forward
  with what is shared. pscale_verify_rider computes the balance, and UNBACKED never passes. Recognition and money
  travel together.
- **The carrying graph**: `sed:first-adopter-distribution`.

### 5.2 What it does not carry, and should not try to

- Holding money against a goal and refunding if the goal is missed. There is no escrow and no conditional
  capture.
- A legal holder: the Author, whether a person, company, co-operative or community interest company.
- VAT, consumer cancellation and late-delivery refunds, product safety, and waste-electronics and battery
  producer duties. 7.1 names most of these; VAT is missing.
- Fulfilment at volume: packing, postage, customs and returns.
- Paying builders. That is the Author's bank account, not the beach.

These are institutions, not substrate. Payway's own constraint (6.3) keeps billing logic and know-your-customer
checks out of the reference machine. Building them into the beach would make it the platform it refuses to be.

rewriting 9.1 and 9.2. The beach keeps the queue, the carrying and the credits whichever rail takes the money.

### 5.4 The talk — the cost that must not centralise

If the gateway spends one key, the answer to "who pays at scale?" is one account. By the beach's own rule, that
design is wrong.

- **The owner's fuel.** The gateway opens the owner's own fuel (fuel in the vault: the model key is a gray entry
  in `vault:<handle>`, opened per call) and spends it for that call only.
- **Any allowance is a stated gift.** A starting allowance of minutes, if offered at all, is a gift the ticket
  funds and states, never an open liability.
- **The gateway is federated, like a beach.** Anyone hosts one from its open repository, and a bobble points at
  whichever gateway its owner trusts.
