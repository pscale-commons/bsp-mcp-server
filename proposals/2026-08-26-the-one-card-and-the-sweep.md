## The one card (function:ahead, rewritten; old text at archive:function:ahead:2026-08-26)

- A card lives ONLY in the holder's own hand, `ahead:<handle>` — piles of
  plain sentences, one doable thing each, born latched to its holder.
- **Pscale 0 is the sentence** — the holder's words, revisable forever, no
  prefix, no trailing machinery.
- **Pscale −1 is the conditions**, stamped by the door, never typed:
  `2` toward (the family the card serves; absent = personal), `3` the
  stamp, `4` the state (standing when absent; held / doing / done / drop),
  `5` dealt-from (absent on a card the holder wrote themself).
- **No mechanism ever parses the sentence.** The aim grammar is deleted,
  not deprecated — there is no fallback parser. A bare string in a pile is
  a legal personal standing card, which is how an unconverted hand keeps
  rendering without a legacy code path: sentence-only cards are a state of
  the model, not an old mode.
- DONE flips the words at 0 and lands the record where field 2 aims (the
  holder's family mirror at the day's rung; `now:<handle>` when unaimed).
  DOING and HELD are one surgical state write each. The tomorrow gesture
  MOVES the card to pile 2 — the pile IS the defer. A commitment to a DATE
  is not a card at all: it is a booking, voiced in the mirror at that day
  (the bookings seam, branch 3).
- **The hand is optional and never a gate** — in the law's own underscore:
  nothing on this beach waits on a card; a card is an EXIT from the hand,
  never the entrance to the now.
