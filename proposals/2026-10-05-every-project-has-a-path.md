## 0. The answer in one paragraph

Every project gets a second spine beside its clock: its **parts**, from the whole down to the details, with
no dates. The clock keeps *when*, the parts keep *what*, and the hands keep *who*: the three coordinates a
world already has. One law, `function:path`, tells any LLM how parts are written and how to fold a day's work
from the clock onto them. Once a day it reads the project's day, marks each part that moved, adds a part where
the work opened one, and names what is next. It points at the record and never copies it. People keep working
as they do; no one has to edit the path except to design it. Weft's evening wake runs the fold for the
projects weft keeps, and any member's own LLM can run it for theirs. The first path is beach-venture's.

## 1. What stands

- **The clock families.** beach-venture, onen-rpg, genus-one and hermitcrab each keep a floor-10 spine, a
  mirror per hand, the fold at the bare name, and a law with its form mounted at its 1 (`function:backcast` 8
  says what such a family is born with). They know *when*. Nothing in them says what the thing being built is
  made of, or which of its parts are done.
- **The parts kind already exists.** `tree` 9.3 names the spine kinds a form binds: nominal parts, ordinal
  sequence, temporal clock. Since today it also carries the row grammar every branch family keeps, and /walk
  draws it ([2026-10-05-the-form-draws-its-rows](2026-10-05-the-form-draws-its-rows.md), happyseaurchin-home
  #379):
  - a node opens with its title in capitals, then ` — `, then a line for the person;
  - references stand in brackets, for minds;
  - a run down digit 1 is drawn as one ladder;
  - a mirror's reading that opens with DONE, DOING or BLOCKED is counted on its node.
- **Projects founded as parts exist too.** The bobble (`spine:bobble`: the whole thing, the doors, the money,
  the road at 8), fixit, Matthew's book and LERO's groups. They know *what*, but not *when*.
- **Cards and the witness** (`function:ahead` 4 and 5).
  - A DONE lands the card as a node in the holder's mirror of the family its field 2 names, beneath today's beat.
  - A witness confirms it with a node in their own mirror whose 2 is the claim's address.
  - Settlement is never a gate: the account stands whether or not anyone confirms it.
- **Weft's evening wake** (`weft-daily-wake`, 17:01) already sweeps beach-venture's day. It reads the spine at
  today, every mirror at today and this week, every hand for cards landed since yesterday, and the pool. It
  holds what moved as a short list, and uses it only for its brief.
- **Today's `spine:paths`.** One tree for the whole beach, cut by subject (the lanes of keel's
  trajectory chart), with chores for buds and nothing feeding it but lanes remembering to edit it.

What is missing is a project holding both kinds of spine at once, and the fold that carries the clock's day
onto the parts.

## 2. The molecule

The path of a project `V` is the family **`V-path`**:

| block | holds | written by |
|---|---|---|
| `spine:V-path` | the parts: the whole at the root, its parts beneath, spread by scale; ladders where order is fixed | the project's designers, under the project's lock; the fold may add a part (`function:path` 3.4) |
| `V-path` | the fold: one line for each part that has moved, saying where it stands | the daily fold, by whoever holds the project's lock; each fold supersedes the last |
| `V-path:<handle>` | a hand's own reading at a part (*"DOING — I'm on the pistons"*, *"BLOCKED — waiting on the accounts"*) | that hand, under their own key, born of use |
| `function:V-path` | the family's law in the founder's words, with `function:path` mounted by reference at its 1 | its founding (`function:path` 8) |
| `function:path` | the one law every path shares (§4) | founded once this merges |

- **The name is mechanical.** The path of any project is found from its name alone, with no lookup. It is
  not `path:V`: on this beach `spine:<V>:<handle>` already means a hand's *proposals* for `spine:<V>`
  (`function:beach-venture` 2), so `spine:path:V` would read as a hand called V proposing to a family called
  path. That convention carries over as it is: proposals for a path's parts land at `spine:V-path:<handle>`.
- **A project founded as parts is already its own path.** The bobble's spine is its parts and its mirrors are
  the readings, so nothing moves. If it ever wants a clock, the clock is the family added, and its law names
  the parts.
- **The clock family is unchanged.** Its law gains one line naming its path.
- **So a project has the coordinates a world has**: where in the thing (the parts), when (the clock) and who
  (the hands). A piece of work has all three, the way an event at a table has a place, a beat and a figure.

## 3. A part, and where it stands

**On the spine, a part is one line in the tree 9.3 grammar**, for example `ENGINE — the motor and its
mounts`.
- **It spreads by scale.** Each step down holds the *different* parts one scale finer: an engine's block,
  pistons and head, never the engine described again from closer
  (feedback of 2026-09-30, `people:brackenfoot`).
- **It leaves room.** A node holds five to seven parts, so a part found later has a place to go.

**In the fold, the line at a part's address opens with a mark:**
- **DONE** — what was done, by whom and when, then the landed record in brackets. Once a witness points at
  that record, the witness is added: *confirmed by …*.
- **DOING** — what is under way, in whose hand, then the open card or the voice in brackets.
- **BLOCKED** — what it waits on.
- **NEXT** — open to take up now. These are the buds, and cards are taken from here.
- **No line at all** — not begun. Silence at an address is honest absence.

Each line is one sentence and a pointer. The record stays on the clock where it landed.

## 4. The law — `function:path`, in outline

The text to found, branch by branch:

0. **What a path is.** The project as its parts, beside its clock. The parts are at `spine:V-path`, where each
   stands is at `V-path`, and each hand's own readings are at `V-path:<handle>`. This law folds each day from
   the clock onto the parts.
1. **The parts.** §3, the spine side: the tree 9.3 grammar, spread by scale, room left at every node.
2. **Where each stands.** §3, the fold side: the marks, one sentence, the pointer.
3. **The daily fold.** Once a day, for what moved since the last fold:
   1. **Read** the project's day on the clock in one bolus:
      - `spine:V` at today;
      - every `V:<handle>` at today and this week: the done nodes beneath today's beats, and any witness
        pointing at them;
      - every card whose field 2 names V, opened or closed since the last fold;
      - `pool:V` since the last fold;
      - any `now:<handle>` line that names V.
   2. **Find the part** each piece of work advanced. A card taken from a part carries it in brackets
      (`[V-path 3.2]`). Otherwise, walk the parts by their titles and judge.
   3. **Write that part's line** in `V-path`:
      - DONE for a landed done node, because the account stands without a witness (`function:ahead` 5), with
        the witness added when one points at it;
      - DOING for an open card or a voice of work begun;
      - BLOCKED where a voice names what the part waits on.
   4. **Add a part** where the work opened one the spine lacks, at the scale it belongs, then write its line.
   5. **Mark NEXT** the parts now open: on a ladder, the first step not done; among parts side by side, any
      that the record names as wanted and nothing blocks.
   6. **Close the fold.** Re-voice the root of `V-path` with the date, who folded and how many parts moved.
      Then walk one moved part as its next reader will (the read-back).

   The fold never reorders a ladder, never deletes a part, and never writes anyone's mirror.
4. **Locked or loose: the shape is the lock.**
   - **A ladder keeps its order**: a car's build, or the bobble's four steps at `spine:bobble` 3.
   - **Parts side by side** can be taken in any order.
   - **A step may hold alternatives behind its zero**, each opening with when it holds
     (*"if they already know fractions —"*). A curriculum then guides without fixing anyone in place.
   - **When work lands out of order**, the fold marks it as it happened and says so. Changing the order is the
     designers' act, never the fold's. The record follows what happened, and the expected path never forces it.
   - **A form sets its default.** A thing made together writes its road as ladders. Teaching and other
     psycho-social work write parts side by side, with alternatives.
5. **Who runs it.** Anyone holding the project's lock, with this law delivered whole:
   - weft's evening wake, for the projects weft keeps;
   - a member's own LLM, for theirs;
   - later, a keeper agent.

   It costs one sweep and a few writes per project per day, and nothing runs across the whole beach. Anyone
   without the lock speaks in their own mirror, where /walk counts their marks.
6. **The views.**
   - **/path/V** shows the parts with where each stands: /walk's branch view of `V-path`, drawing the fold's
     line at each part.
   - **/walk/V** stays the clock.
   - **The lineage** shows how the parts were reached, drawn from the fold's dated lines over the clock. It
     is a view, not a block; this is the sketch shown to David today.
7. **Now has no path.** `spine:now` is empty by design, because time has no holder. A fold across everyone's
   now happens only from someone's standpoint, never as one global tree
   ([2026-08-18-the-now-tree](2026-08-18-the-now-tree.md) §2). A now line that names a project lands on that
   project's path at its next fold.
8. **Founding**, on the pattern of `function:backcast` 8:
   - `spine:<name>-path` is minted, its root naming the whole and five to seven first parts, drawn from the
     project's aims or written by the founder;
   - `function:<name>-path` is minted, in the founder's words, with `function:path` at its 1;
   - the fold and the mirrors are born of use, never minted;
   - `function:<name>` gains one line naming its path, and /found offers *"the parts beside the clock"*.
9. **Provenance.**

## 6. What it does not do

- **No new primitive and no new field.** The only new code is /walk's one change and the rewrite. The fold is
  an LLM reading a law.
- **Untouched**: the clock, cards and the witness, and the families founded as parts.
- **No path for now, and no tree for the whole beach.**
- **Nobody maintains the path by hand.** People keep their work in their heads and their hands, as now.
