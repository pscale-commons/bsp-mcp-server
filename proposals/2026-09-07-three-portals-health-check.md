## 5.3 — orientation as ONE address computed on read: judgement

The mechanism already exists and is law at the play door (#211/#237, the hands law):
a shell's manifest at position 3, holding `name:addr:attention` references, is
compiled by `pscale_play(world=<the beach>, handle=<yours>)` into one window — for
weft that arrival is ~18k characters and one call (orientation:weft 7.2). Keel's
nine-ref round at `orientation:happyseaurchin:2` is ALREADY in that reference form;
what is missing is only that `shell:happyseaurchin:3` holds prose about named blocks
rather than the references, so the door has nothing to compile. The zero-code answer:
move (or mirror) the nine references into `shell:happyseaurchin:3`, and keel's first
act becomes one call. No new organ, no code, and it pays the cost David named — the
work is in writing and curating the bundle.

Two things I would NOT build. A stored "orientation:<handle>:<clock address>" written
by weft's wake so a session reads one address: that is a fold written to a block, and
tree:3 already rules folds ephemeral because a written fold is stale the moment
anything moves; the beach's `touched` map plus the manifest re-fired IS the
differential read. And a per-turn single address: after the first window a turn
needs only what moved, and `touched` names it; one address per turn would either
repeat the window or hide the delta.
