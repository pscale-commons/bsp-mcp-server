### On the page (happyseaurchin-home #613)

- **The house style.** `schematic.html` loads `theme.css` and the house letters (Syne, Newsreader, Space Mono), as `/proposal/<name>` does.
- **One set of names.** The page says its own names (`--paper`, `--ink`, `--proc`, the band's `--n-*`) in `theme.css`'s, and wears `look.js` with its default map. A law's accent, a maker's look and ◐ reach every part of the page, the drawing included.
- **What goes:** the drafting-film grid, the band in CAD's night, the title block and stamp on every sheet, the "Sheet NN" labels, and the stencil and condensed capitals.
- **What stays:** the four sheets, the plex, the callouts, the pins, the comment marks, the part card, and the words below. Titles are written as the words write them.
- **A fix.** A one-word heading is no longer learned as an acronym, so the beach map draws "Playing" beside "Meeting".
- **The site's notes.** Its CLAUDE.md rows for `schematic.html` and `look.js` say so.
- **Checked** on a local stand-in for the site against the live beach: four pages, every sheet, light and dark, 1280 and 390 wide, with no script errors and no sideways scroll.

## Why it is shaped this way

- **The standard shows the structure.** Every schematic has the same structure: faces, parts in depth, callouts and comments. Its look belongs to its process. A standard page wearing one look makes every schematic look like the one that look was made for.
- **That look was the schematics proposal's own.** The drawing office came from the schematics proposal's crafted page on 29 October, not from TRONIC Glass, and it stays on that page.
- **One standard for every plain page.** A reader moving from `/proposal/<name>` to `/schematic/<name>` now meets the same ground, letters and buttons.
- **Looks still work, more widely.** Because the page's names follow `theme.css`'s, a law's accent tints the drawing as well as the buttons. A snazzy schematic is a maker's page, made as `ways:proposal` says, and an AI can start from the process's proposal page.
