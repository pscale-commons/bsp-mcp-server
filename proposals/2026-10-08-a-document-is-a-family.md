# A document is a family — 2026-10-08

David, 8 October 2026, after a day run from the beach as weft's desk produced two proposal blocks he could only read as a flat page: "Documents are humans' attempt to coordinate whatever is in our heads collectively so that we act together. But they can become the thing itself rather than the actual human coordination … I want minimal structures so that we as humans are actually maximally living." And the ask: "Can't they be read as /walk as a doc reader so that I can write my comments in mirrors?"

## The ruling

1. **A proposal is a family, never a bare block.** `spine:<name>` carries the text; `function:<name>` carries the law with `mode: document` at its 1; a reading lands in `<name>:<handle>` at the same address, never in the spine; the fold at the bare name is read, never owned. The shared law is `function:document`, listed in the library at `tree` 9.2 beside the venture, the question set, the progression, the health board, the neighbourhood of gifts and the thing made together.

2. **The form is named in the law, confirmed by the structure, and stays a view.** The mode is the word after `mode:` at the law's position 1, a line in a pscale block like any other. The walk page reads it by the read it already makes of `function:<name>`; where the law says nothing it falls back to the structure (a floor-10 spine is a clock, otherwise branches). A document and a tree of parts have the same structure, so only the law can say "one voice, read me down". A switch on the page may show any family in any dialect.

3. **Author a document as a spindle, not a fan.** One line at the root, the first step at 1, the next at 1.1 and so down (sequence is depth, 2026-09-24); a step's other digits are its side-rooms, opened only when wanted. The complicated version fans seven branches of five; the complex one runs down one spindle with its detail nested. The two first instances were re-cut this way the same day: `autumn-revenue` (from `proposal:autumn-revenue`) and `character-sheets` (from `proposal:character-sheets`), each spine under the shared project lock so David corrects it directly, the old blocks re-voiced at their roots as pointers.

4. **A document is set aside when read.** Once the keeper's reading stands at the root, or the decision it asked for is taken, it is copied to `archive:spine:<name>:<date>` and the living lines (the hand's day, the venture's week, the path's part) carry what it decided. A document is never the thing itself.

## The reader (happyseaurchin-home, built the same day)

The walk gains a fourth dialect beside clock, branches and ground: **document**. Vertical; the run down digit 1 is the page; each step at full length with its side-rooms collapsed beneath it, opening in place on one tap together with their own children; every hand's reading at that address beside the step, and the say box for a handle in the URL; nothing fixed in width or height. A docs row of tabs under the projects row lists the families whose law says document, with an address box to open any family by name (the editor's "load beach", on the walk). A view switch shows any family as document, columns (the existing rack) or the raw walk; the default comes from the law.
