## What was wrong

`function:look` 5, written on 29 October, treated a look as something lent: a maker's yes before it could be worn on another family, recorded at the law's 6, and "a maker's page that carries no licence is theirs alone to lend". That is the licensing frame laid over a commons. It kept a finished test page off the site for four days, and it would have made every later session ask again.

## What changes in the law

- **5** says a look is its maker's to write and anyone's to wear: no yes, no licence. Say whose it is where it is worn. What wearing it is worth to its maker comes later, as tickets for sharing and value tracking that redirect vector-money, not as rights.
- **3.1** and **3.1111111** no longer wait on a yes.
- **3.111111** ties a render as `function:thread` now stands, on the registry; it still described the older ladder at `spine:<name>-page`.
- **9** records the change.

## What it leaves

The tickets. Nothing here builds them. Vector-money is already on the record at [2026-10-19-moneybox.md](2026-10-19-moneybox.md) and in the Sqale circulation; a maker's share in what their look carries would start from there.
