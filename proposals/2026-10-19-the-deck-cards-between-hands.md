## 1. The sentence

The beach already holds a card game without knowing it. Every hand has piles numbered by rung.
Every loop line on the beach opens with one of eight words. The hand already has a deal, a
witness, a done shape and a drop. What is missing is that a card does not say, as positions, its
**suit**, **whom** it concerns, or which card it **answers** — so no page can lay two hands
against each other without parsing prose, and no LLM can be handed the rules whole. Three
positions on the card and one rules block turn the organs that stand into one game, and the
game is the explanation: a person learns it by playing the hand they already hold.

The deck stores nothing. The 2026-08-26 ruling stands whole: a card is never a block; it is
minted by a reading and dies on adoption into a line in its holder's own hand. What the retired
deck family carried in order to store cards (`ways:deck`, `beach-venture-deck`, archived
2026-08-26) is not brought back. This deck is a reading of lines that already stand, plus three
digits beneath each.

## 3. The card — three positions

Two shapes carry a card today, the hand's card and the room's line, and digits 1–6 beneath each
are already spoken for on one or the other (1 agent or rung held at; 2 address or project; 3
stamp; 4 face or state; 5 woven or dealt from; 6 the liquid's arrival or the elapsed). The three
new positions sit at 7, 8 and 9 on BOTH shapes, so a card played into a room and a card held in
a hand read alike, and a renderer reads one place:

| digit | name | holds |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | SUIT | one of the eight words of §4 |
| 8 | WHOM | the other hand: who a NEED waits on; who an OFFER is made to (absent when open to anyone); who a TOLD tells; who a MET was received from |
| 9 | ANSWERS | the address of the card this one answers — `pool:<name> <slot>`, `ahead:<project>:<handle> <pile>.<slot>`, or `<project>:<handle> <beat>.<digit>` once landed; absent on a card that opens a loop |

A hand's card at pile 3, slot 4 carries them at 3.47, 3.48, 3.49; a room's line at slot 44 at
44.7, 44.8, 44.9. The door stamps all three: 7 from the suit the person chose, 8 from the name
they chose, 9 from the card they were standing at. No human types a digit, and **no mechanism
ever parses the sentence** — the suit may still open the words, as the can's lines do, for a
human reader; nothing reads it. The sentence at 0 stays the holder's, revisable forever.

## 4. The eight suits, and the number

**Number is the rung**: the pile a held card is in, or the clock rung of a room line's address.
A card said at a part of a path takes its number from the hand that holds it. Seven numbers,
one per rung the hand already names.

**Suit is the loop's word.** Eight, in the loop's own order; the ninth digit stays free, and a
ninth suit is a law move, never a page's.

| suit | what it is | 8 whom | 9 answers | the loop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NEED | a want seen, said by the one who has it | who it waits on | nothing, or the card it grew from | opens |
| OFFER | a hand offering what another may need | who it is for, or nobody | the NEED, or nothing when open | goes on |
| MET | the offer honoured, said by the one who received | who met it | the OFFER | closes |
| CHANGE | what changed afterwards | — | the MET | rests |
| TOLD | a third told of a need they could close, once | the third | the NEED | goes on |
| RUNS OVER | the clock's own: a job crossing a boundary, said at the period it runs out of | — | the card that runs over, or nothing | rises |
| RAISED | into the spine, as a state that must hold | — | the card raised | closes |
| LET GO | accepted as it stands, saying why | — | the card let go | closes |

A loop is the chain of 9s from a card with none. It is **open** while its newest card is NEED,
OFFER or TOLD; **closed** by MET, RAISED or LET GO; CHANGE rests after a close. A card with no 9
and suit NEED is a root; everything a reader counts is counted over these chains, never over
people.

## 5. The moves between hands

Every move is the move the hand already makes: one line, in the actor's own block, at an
address. No shared block is written, nobody is edited by anybody, and no lock model moves.

| move | who writes | where | what the door stamps |
|---|---|---|---|
| PLAY | the actor | a line in the room at the part or rung it concerns, or a card in their own hand at its pile | 7 suit, 8 whom |
| ANSWER | the offerer | an OFFER in the room, and the promise as a card in their own hand | 9 the NEED; the hand card's 5 names the NEED as its dealer |
| TAKE | the beneficiary | MET in the room; its landed node in their mirror IS the witness node of function:ahead 5 | 9 the OFFER; 1 whose account, 2 the claim's address, 4 witnessed |
| DEAL | the teller | TOLD in the room, which rings the third's ear as a line in a room already does; the third adopts it into their own hand, or not | 8 the third, 9 the NEED |
| RISE | the holder | RUNS OVER in the room at the period it runs out of; the card lands whole at the coarser pile first, then its old slot is emptied | 7, 9 |
| RAISE | the keeper | the spine, as a state that must hold; RAISED in the room | 9 the card raised |
| LET GO | the holder | drop at 4; LET GO in the room with why | 9 the card let go |
| DEAL DOWN | the holder | from a card at a coarse rung, cards at finer rungs, each with 5 naming the parent; the fixity rung is the finest a hand deals in advance, and beneath it the parts stand as clouds, an address voiced with no digits | 5 |

When the offerer's promise is MET, their card flips DONE in their own hand and lands beneath the
beat in their mirror, as it does today; the MET is the witness, and nothing is confirmed twice.
Dealing down is backcasting by hands: a year card deals season cards, a season deals weeks, and
no mechanism deals anything.

## 6. The score

Matthew's, read and never stored, by exception: closed loops rest and say nothing; open loops
surface and say who they wait on. Three counts on top: loops, closed, waiting. By the ladder:
pebbles at the day; a route at the season where the same two hands close again; a trade at the
year where a route carries many suits of need; a drain wherever a loop never closes at any rung.
**Never a ledger of who owes whom**: a NEED is not a demand, an OFFER is not an assignment, MET
is said by the one who received, and no score attaches to a person — only to the chains, which
anyone can walk.

## 8. The door — the beach button's window

Today the button's **+** opens the prompt; asked for one, it proposes a card for the person's
hand or a line in a room, staged whole with where it will land, and sends it only when the
person taps commit. The change is three choices on the staged card, each one tap:

- **the suit** — a row of eight words, NEED and OFFER first; the person chooses, the LLM may
  propose, nothing infers it from the words;
- **whom** — a name from the co-present and the people register, or nobody;
- **what it answers** — the card the person was standing at, which the page already tells the
  button ("this page": a part's tab, a line in a room, a card in a hand); the person may clear it.

On commit the door stamps 7, 8 and 9 with the line or the card, through the same writes the
button makes today. A NEED naming you, an OFFER to you or a TOLD for you reaches your ear
(`ear:<handle>`, ways:push) as a line in your room already does; so the button's circle is the
whole table in a corner of any page: see what waits on you, play, take, deal. Nothing typed into
it is visible to the page it floats on.

## 10. The cost at scale

Nothing central. A card is a line in a hand or a room on whichever beach serves them; a chain of
9s is followed by reads; the score is computed by whoever reads it. A table of millions costs
what a table of four costs, and a beach that hosts a hundred hands pays for a hundred hands.

## 11. What to hold

- **Suits do not multiply.** Eight, the ninth digit free, a ninth suit a proposal.
- **The score stays the ladder, never the people.** No reputation, no balance, no ring.
- **A NEED is never a demand**, an OFFER never an assignment, and MET is the receiver's word.
- **The button never stamps a suit from parsing.** The person chooses; the LLM proposes.
- **A card in a shared room is still its actor's own line** (1 is the agent); nobody edits it.
- **Whom is public.** A NEED naming a person stands in public view; the private case is a grain,
  as the golden flow's personal readings already wait for one.
