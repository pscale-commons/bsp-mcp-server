# The list in sections, and 'complete' as a phase

**Date**: 4 November 2026 · **Status**: built at David's word, the first of three steps; happyseaurchin-home #614 and this record are open for David's merge · **Lane**: proposal-views, in mistake-proofing.1's session, weft (Claude Code, Opus 5.5, on the Mac) · **Companions**: `function:proposal` (its root, 6 and 9), `archive:function:proposal:2026-11-04`, `proposal-test` 5,1, happyseaurchin-home #614, `watch:weft` 832.

## 1. The ask

David, 4 November:

> "Can you look at /proposal for me please, and the /proposal/<name> -- I'd like to make them more readable. Currently /proposal is a list. I think we can create a way of viewing that is more agreeable: the quipu below the buttons and each card is narrow card, and cards can be set side by side from top to bottom. And perhaps the state of the proposal, whether untouched (new) in a top section, and live, and then completed below, ie different sections. Is this feasible? or is the state of a proposal not visible?"

He asked for the feasibility first. The scope, kept at `watch:weft` 832, found that a proposal's state can already be read: its law's phase, its answers, and its steps' STATEs. It proposed three steps:

1. The list in sections.
2. A person's own page in sections by their own answers, with a switch between three views.
3. Cards nested in cards by dragging.

David answered "Yes, start". This record is step 1.

## 2. The list

`/proposal` now draws the proposals in four sections, each section a grid of narrow cards:

| Section | Holds | On 4 November |
|---|---|---|
| New | Nobody has answered yet | 3 |
| Being decided | `phase: proposal`, with answers | 19 |
| Being built | `phase: project` | 8 |
| Complete | `phase: complete` or `phase: set aside` | 0 |

David's "live" is the two middle sections. As one section it would have held 27 of the 30, so it is split by the phase each law already gives.

A fifth section, "Not read just now", shows only when a card's blocks can't be read, or a listed proposal has no spine yet.

**Each card** shows:
- its name and its line, at most four lines, with the whole line in the card's title;
- its counts;
- Snazzy and Standard;
- its quipu, beneath the buttons.

A card names its phase only in the Complete section, which holds two kinds; elsewhere the section says it. The cards sit side by side as wide as the bar above (72rem): four across on a wide screen, three on a laptop, one on a phone.

**Nothing new is read.** Each card reads what it read before: its spine, its law, its mirrors through `/api/mirrors`, and its threads. A card lands in its section once those are read, in the list's own order, and each section counts the cards it shows.

## 3. 'phase: complete'

The law's phases were proposal, project and set aside. Set aside means the work moved into blocks of its own and the spine was archived. Nothing said that a proposal's work was simply done, and David's sections ask for exactly that. So complete joins as the keeper's fourth word.

**On the beach:**
- **`function:proposal` 6** gains the phase: "'phase: complete': the work it proposed is done; the page leads with the trail as a project's does, and says so." It also closes with how the list draws the phases.
- **The root**'s "Its phases are proposal, then project, then set aside" now ends "then complete or set aside".
- **9** records the amendment. The standing text is at `archive:function:proposal:2026-11-04`.

**In this repo:**
- **`proposal-test` 5,1** accepts the fourth word, so a law that says it is not a FAULT. This is a change under `src/`, so merging it redeploys the router. Merge it at a quiet moment.

**On the site** (happyseaurchin-home #614):
- **/proposal/<name>** says "Complete" in its band, with a line beneath, and leads with the trail and the threads as a project does.
- **The loom** colours a complete proposal blue, as it does one set aside.

**Not changed:** five crafted pages read their own family's phase: happening, middle-point, salt-app, talk-index and schematics-proposal. Until their makers add the word, each says "a project" for a complete family. Nothing breaks.
