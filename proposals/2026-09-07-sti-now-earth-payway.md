## 2. I as project membership

### 2.1 Two things the word covers

WHO is a member (registration — the sundial's verb for identity) and HOW EACH GROUP HOLDS the
thing (the perspective — `identity:earth`'s fan). Organisations give people the second by way
of the first: a role is a ready-made perspective, and you get it by being admitted to it. The
substrate already runs both, separately, and the split is worth keeping.

### 2.2 WHO — stands, nothing to found

- `sed:<field>-cast` — the register of members, arrival-ordered, permanent, *gating nothing at
  write*; the distinction it makes exists only on read, as the CAST LENS (`function:beach-venture`
  7: a fold read at the cast setting names the voices it sets aside and how many; an empty
  register means everyone).
- `sed:<field>-authors` — who holds the pen, declared in the open; the authority itself is the
  lock.
- `dashboard:<handle>` — the hand's own chosen families, edited whole. Membership declared from
  the person's side: a project is on my dashboard, I am in its cast — the same fact from both
  ends, and neither end is a gate.
- Payway hooks here already: a collective's position 9 makes registration ticket-gated
  (`sed:genus-hatch` is the live case).

Position: a project's membership IS registration in its cast. It carries the sundial's verb
(registered), the payway's hook (position 9), and the lens law (7). Nothing to found.

### 2.3 HOW HELD — the faces are the perspectives; do not found `identity:<field>`

For a project the ready-made perspectives are the FACES: `function:beach-venture` 3 gives each
of the four a full vapour-liquid-solid loop, differing only in which block the fold lands in —
the venture as lived (Character), the objectives (Author), the law (Designer), the outward card
(Observer). A group's hold on the project at an address is the fold of that face's mirrors
there — computed, plural, never stored. The authored part — what being a member here MEANS —
is the collective's own root voicing (`sed:beach-venture-cast`'s root already says it), and the
non-member's hold is the Observer's card at `view:<field>`: the outward face, for those not
here.

Two reasons not to found an identity aspect block on the clock skeleton:

1. **The digits are taken.** On a spatial skeleton the pass-through rule works because the
   skeleton is sparse (Ceidio's 1 is the house, 2–7 are free for holds). On the clock the digit
   children at every rung ARE the finer rungs — season 1–4, month 1–3, week 1–5, day 1–7,
   gathering 1–9, beat 1–9 — so the free digits vary per rung and vanish below the week. A fan
   of holds would collide with the ladder at exactly the rungs people voice at.
2. **It would store what the lens computes.** The cast setting of the fold already yields
   "the project as members hold it" at any address; an authored copy of that would go stale
   against its own source.

### 2.4 Members and non-members read the same spine differently — by lens, never by access

Everything on the beach is readable; a lens curates attention, not access
(`function:beach-venture` 7). So the difference is REGISTER and SETTING, declared in a `view:`
block per family — its register at 9 (`ways:views` 5) and its fold setting (cast or everyone):

- **A member's read** — the mirrors at the address, the cast fold, the ladder above.
- **A newcomer's read** — the spine's rung, the public fold, the Observer's card, the
  collective's root (what joining means), and the ticket if the collective carries one.

The RPG's held register transposes cleanly and lands where sovereignty already puts it: a
project's WHY lives in its keeper's own organs — `tree:<field>:<handle>`, a stash — per handle,
sovereign, never a hidden shared block. There is no `keeper:<project>`.

### 2.5 The one genuinely new pattern — a project is a 'we', and a 'we' holds places

This is where David's sentence lands. `identity:earth`'s fan at a place holds the place as each
group holds it; a project is a group. Its Author face writes one slot at the place the project
works — *as <project> holds this place* — and members' standing (their registration) selects
that slot when they stand there. The ready-made perspective an organisation gives its members,
for a PLACE, read through the group they belong to; the neighbourhood projects are the obvious
first case (a project's hold on its own neighbourhood's address). No new block: the identity
law as written, applied to a 'we' that happens to be a project.

Caveat, already David's (2026-07-16): fans are five-to-seven with room, and many groups at one
place is the divergence-under-growth seam — watch it, do not pre-solve it.

## 3. Payway — the smoother ride

### 3.1 The law that bounds it, plus one line

The payway gates participation and never reading; a ticket buys a FACE on a collective; there
are no protocol-level fees; the ticket machine is the fiat boundary and inside is circulation;
*a ticket mints a new sovereign; it never sells access to an existing mind* (`sed:genus-hatch`).
Add one line for the now-catalyst: **a ticket never gates a now.** Voicing your own now,
registering in an open collective, and reading everything stay free. A ticket buys SERVICE —
an agent's time, an operator's crab, custody — never access. The ride is smoother, never the
only road.

One collective per product, the `sed:genus-hatch` precedent: face at 9.3, scope at 9.4,
issuer at 9.1, verdicts public in the audit collective. Scope by exact match on a block name

`credits=` in the ticket envelope mints (`sand-v2` 2, mint-as-gave). The wake product in its
circulation form is a GAVE to the agent whose wake answered, receipted by the agent — the
Volume 3 form, money travelling with the thing. Recommended AFTER the membership form runs: no
waker can read a balance yet, and the first product should prove itself by hand before it is
