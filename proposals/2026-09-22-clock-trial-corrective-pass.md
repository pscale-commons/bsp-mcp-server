## 4. What broke instead — none of it in the zoom

1. **The day ended five times.** The lean voiced the-day *"the last grey drains out of the sky"*
   at 155, 156 and 158 over a ford that has been dark since 153. A stateless lean seat reads the
   fold and the held side but the law never tells it to read **a figure's own mirror** before
   voicing that figure. One clause fixes it: *before voicing a figure, read its mirror at the
   last folded address — a figure continues from its own last line.*
2. **The day's fold re-told beat 158** — the ale line, the slack rope. This is weft's own
   amendment over-correcting: *"begin from exactly where the last finer fold left everyone and
   carry it forward"* was read as re-telling. One clause: *begin from it in a sentence; never
   re-tell what a finer fold already holds.*
3. **A fold paraphrased a said act** — Hobb's *"moves with the pull of the current"* became
   *"easy against the pull"* — and Hobb's telling of 158 added a closing paragraph the fold did
   not hold. The verbatim law stands in 1.3 and 2; it was not kept. Seat craft, not law.
4. **Tempo.** *The finer rung pulls* kept the beat live for four beats while the place's people
   walked away one line at a time; the judge: "M6–M8, where three moments deliver one." The lean
   let the world go slowly. 2.2 already lets a fold close the gathering when the scene has
   plainly ended, and 158 used it; the lean's law should say the same — *when the characters
   withdraw, let the world withdraw in one line, not three.*
5. **Fog, in both arms.** Each player is told what their character could not see. This is
   track A's to fix as much as B's: the telling's law says *from where the character stands*
   and *whole, nothing added*, and the two pull apart; David rules which wins.
