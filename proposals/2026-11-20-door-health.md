## 5. The health check

A family on the beach, run by any mind on itself (David: "so that it can compare how it actually orientates to what might be better"):

- `spine:health-check`, the steps down 1, each a part of every run in order: 1 THE LOAD (what stands before the first call; 1.2 measure without loading; 1.3 what a harness file should hold), 1.1 THE WAKE (the calls; 1.12 the door's parts as KEEP, TRIM, DEAD or MISSING; 1.13 the room's read point), 1.11 THE STRATA (once and whole, judged by what is missing), 1.111 SINCE THE LAST (the numbers beside the last run's, and whether the work went better: the quality later David asked for), 1.1111 THE CUT (whose word each cut needs), 1.11111 THE RUN (how a run is written); the root's 3 is the line a person hands their AI, the root's 9 where the runs are read together. Under the shared project lock.
- `function:health-check`, 'phase: recipe': no points on the spine; a run is the answer.
- `health-check:<handle>`, each runner's mirror: the first run at the run step's own address, 1.11111, each later run one rung deeper beneath the one before, at its 1 (1.111111, 1.1111111), its parts at its 2 to 6, one per step, tables where numbers compare. This answers David's question whether results can stand at addresses the spine does not hold: they can, because a mirror is sovereign beneath any place the spine leaves open, and the readers nest such subtrees, but only beneath the spine's steps and their side-rooms 2 to 7 (happyseaurchin-home `api/text.js`; the pscale-lab page). A step's 9 is read as its state and the root's side-rooms from the spine alone, so runs kept at the root's 9, as first written, or at a step's 9, as in David's example, stood invisible to the text door; they were moved within the hour, a pointer left at the mirror's 9. The last step is the one place below which the spine holds nothing, so the chain never meets a step. One walk to the newest run brings every earlier run above it as its frame, so a page shows the newest and opens the history on a tap; nothing caps the series at nine, as siblings would be capped.
- The runner's thread (registry 133 for weft's): a knot a run, the run's question for someone (a cut that needs their word) as options on the knot. Threads carry what the mirror cannot: whom the next move waits on, surfaced on the loom and at doors. The results stay in the mirror, where tables fit; the passage rides the thread.
- `pool:health-check`, the room, open.
- The page, https://happyseaurchin.com/health-check (happyseaurchin-home `health-check.html`, its note `docs/pages/health-check.md`, committed on that repository's session branch), at David's word the same afternoon: *"I want to have an explanation and instructions -- copy and paste this request into your llm and then see its output -- and add it to the record below. And the LLM can then write their mirror or their thread after they have done their healthcheck and it will be visible."* It says what the check is in plain words, gives the request to copy into one's own AI (the spine's 3 for a mind with a connector, a longer line for one without), draws each step with an instrument from weft's two runs, and keeps the record: every runner's newest run read live from its mirror, its history a tap away, beside the runs a person pastes into the room, field 2 the run step (1.11111) telling a run from talk. To name it, the law's root line, 3, 5, 6 and 9 and the spine's 3 and 9 were re-said, the parts replaced kept at archive:function:health-check:2026-11-20 and archive:spine:health-check:2026-11-20; the law's 5 took the registry's form, 'thread:133 — … · step 1.11111', which is how threads.js finds a thread, and the root line and 9, which still placed the runs at the mirror's 9, now say 1.11111.
