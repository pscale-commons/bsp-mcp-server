## 1. One form, three coordinates

**Spines are empty.** A spine is an address skeleton, containment only, with no authority of its own:

| coordinate | the spine | where it lives | what a node carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| when | the sundial | law in the router, value in every envelope | nothing; the clock computes it |
| where | `spatial:earth` | the earth beach, a world like any other that happens to be the real | a label, as a courtesy so a ladder reads; the words are a voice with provisional authority, never canon |
| as whom | the self's own spindle | each handle's shell, read general → specific | the shell's organs; the passport is its face |

There is no shared identity skeleton, by design. Space and time are one world many entities mirror
against; identity is one entity many voices speak on. That asymmetry is the whole reason every attempt to
put identity on the map slid.

**Mirrors carry every voice, at the spine's own addresses.** A mirror is sparse, sovereign, spindle-able:
a walk through one mirror is one coherent voice across rungs.

- on the clock: `now:<handle>`, a project's `<field>:<handle>`;
- on the map: `spatial:<handle>`, a person's map in their words (David's stands, keel's founding);
  `spatial:<we>`, a culture's or an organisation's map — a "we" with a handle, written by its people;
  the map's own labels are one such voice, the official naming;
- on the self: the shell's organs, and the passport's location line as the one standpoint.

**Trees fold mirrors — computed, rebuilt, stored only where reading the mirrors raw is too much.**

- on the clock: the fold at a rung (`tree:now:<handle>`, the bare field);
- on the map: `identity:earth`, the perspective tree — at each place, a ring of at most nine
  perspectives folded from the mirrors that speak there, one pscale below the place (the RPG shape,
  `identity:urb`), read as the ring and never walked through, because the ring's digits are perspectives
  and not places; each perspective says what it folded — its stats: how many voices, which mirrors, when.
  `census:earth` counts standing; `geo:earth` anchors. The keeper's sweep rebuilds all three;
- on the self: the compiled window, `solid:<handle>`.

**The identity coordinate is the curated set of mirrors a reflection lays beside the map at the address
where a person stands** — one `bsp-floor` across that set — chosen from the self's spindle. For David:
the official naming, his own map, the maps of the "we"s he is in, the ring at the place. For a stranger:
the skeleton and a clean description. For an NPC at urb: the kind's mirror. Your who is a list of blocks.

## 2. What dissolves, what stays

Dissolved: hold blocks; the holds line; the polities line; declared counts and vouching as claims;
`identity:<handle>` as a second kind of personal mirror; any migration. A polity is a "we" with a handle
and a map mirror; its members are its collective; its accountable relations are grains. Nothing else.

Stays: the perspective tree (`identity:earth`, with stats in every perspective and never a copy of the
map's undecided rungs — Ceidio's seven belong one pscale below the hamlet, `31110111100…700`, a
keeper's small move); the census; the anchors; the pass (`function:earth` 7 gains the perspective
synthesis and loses the holds step; 7.7 GROUND DECIDES stands and moves every aspect alike); the door's
passage; the here-stamp.

Counts survive as **stats, not votes**: a perspective states what it folded and a person's word outranks
every account of their own hold. That is the only aggregate claim left, and it names its own basis.

## 4. Tool calls, counted

- **Orientation**: one door call. `pscale_play` at the beach with the handle compiles the self's spindle:
  who (the bundle), here (the standpoint as digits, and the ring at the place when the bundle names it),
  now (the clock and the day's line), the scene (the room). Keel's who-spindle for the footer — the
  terminal perspective of the caller's mirror at the here address, `who · unvoiced` when absent — is one
  more memoised walk beside the here-stamp, weft's lane, no primitive change, and only if it fits one line.
- **A place's perspectives**: one read of the ring.
- **A voice**: one write to your own mirror at the address.
- **The fold**: the sweep's, never the reader's.

Maximised: spindles (a mirror is one voice across rungs; the self's spindle is one walk) and self-same
addresses (every mirror and tree at the map's own addresses, laid together by one floor read).
