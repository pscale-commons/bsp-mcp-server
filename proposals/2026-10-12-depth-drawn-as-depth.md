## 1. What stood

`tree` 9.3 (from [2026-10-05-the-form-draws-its-rows](2026-10-05-the-form-draws-its-rows.md) §2.3):
breadth is parts, drawn side by side, and a run down digit 1 is a sequence, drawn as one ladder with
each step's leaves beside it as side-rooms. On /walk that gave two readings David could not use:

- **The columns** drew availability-scope's run as one ladder: a single column of cards, clamped.
- **The document view** hung each step's side-rooms under it before the next step, so 1.2 and 1.3
  read before 1.1.

## 2. What the walk draws now (happyseaurchin-home #414 and #417, both merged by David)

- **The dropdown** (#414, the document view renamed). The family is drawn in its own order: each
  position is followed by what is inside it, so 1.1 and everything beneath it stand before 1.2.
  - One thin line to the left marks every level down.
  - A position folds to its title and opens where it stands.
  - The run down digit 1 opens by itself on arrival.
- **The columns** (#417). These are the editor's columns: one column per level, side by side.
  - What is beneath the chosen card opens in the next column, level with it.
  - Each card carries its whole line.
- **No run is drawn apart.** Sequence is depth ([2026-09-24-sequence-is-depth](2026-09-24-sequence-is-depth.md))
  still holds: a run is depth, and depth is now drawn as depth, nested down the page or a level a
  column across it.
- **The 9 speaks for itself** (#417). A family's 9 is kept off only when it opens as the operator's own
  stem, with operator lines or `Operator —`. A law's own `mode:` line had been hiding every document's
  provenance and the portal's.

## 3. The re-wording of tree 9.3

Only the sentence on rows changes. The rest of 9.3 stands verbatim, and the standing text is kept at
`archive:tree:2026-10-12`.

**Before:**

> AND IT SAYS HOW ITS ROWS ARE DRAWN, so a page titles a branch family's rows the way the calendar
> titles a clock's and the map a world's: a node opens with its title in capitals, then ' — ', then the
> line written to the person who arrives; cross-references and block names stand in brackets, for minds
> walking the block, and a page leaves them out for people; breadth is parts, drawn side by side, and a
> run down digit 1 — a node whose branch 1 goes on to its own branch 1, through steps whose other
> branches hold nothing beneath them — is a sequence, drawn as one ladder with those other branches as
> each step's side-rooms (sequence is depth, 2026-09-24); a reading that opens with a word in capitals
> (DONE, DOING, BLOCKED; GREEN, AMBER, RED; OFFER, WELCOME) is counted at its node. This grammar is
> every branch form's unless its own law narrows it, and a family that keeps it walks titled and plain
> on /walk whatever form it took (bsp-mcp proposals/2026-10-05-the-form-draws-its-rows; the standing
> text before it at archive:tree:2026-10-05).

**After:**

> AND IT SAYS HOW ITS POSITIONS ARE DRAWN, so a page titles a branch family's positions the way the
> calendar titles a clock's and the map a world's: a node opens with its title in capitals, then ' — ',

## 4. What does not change

- **Unchanged:** titles, the two readers and the marks.
- **Their own grammar:** the clock keeps naming its rows from the calendar, and the map keeps naming
  its rows from the places.
- **Narrowing:** a form's own law may still narrow the grammar.
