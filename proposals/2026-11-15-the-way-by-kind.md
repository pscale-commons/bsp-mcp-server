## The ask

Since 2 November a law whose root line closes `is ways:<name>.` has that way printed beside it, once a session, by `bsp()`, `pscale_stream_engage` and `pscale_play` alike (`proposals/2026-11-02-the-read-hands-over-the-way.md`). On 11 November only one law of 160 did that: `function:thread`. So a mind reading any other family was handed nothing about what to do with it (`spine:o-page-unit` 1.11114).

The o-page unit's answer (1.1111, 1.11111) is that every page a person is handed is the face of one family, and the family's law already says its kind: its 1 the mode (`mode: document`), and for a document its 7 the phase (`phase: proposal`, `inquiry`, `recipe` and so on). Each of those names a form, `function:<mode>` or `function:<phase>`. Set a kind's way down once, at its form, and every family of that kind is handed it.

## What changes

**One rule, in one place** (`wayHandedOver`, `src/tools/stream.ts`). Where a family's own law names no way:

1. read its 7 for `phase: <p>` and its 1 for `mode: <m>`;
2. ask `function:<p>`, then `function:<m>`, each at the family's own beach and then at the default beach, and take the first form whose root closes `is ways:<x>.`;
3. hand that way over as before: once a session, its root and steps without the provenance, read from the beach where the form was found.

A law naming its own way keeps it. A phase with no form of its name (`set aside` reads as `set`) falls to the mode. A law with neither line is handed nothing. What the forms answered is kept beside what was given, for the same two hours and with the same bound, so a session asks the forms once (`kindWays`, beside `standing`). The three callers are unchanged: the stream after the law, `pscale_play` for a thread's law, `bsp()` for an opening append at a spine's root.

**On the beach, the same day** (no code):

- `ways:reader`, the reader's way, one for every page (keel's CHANGE at 1.18, taken by weft): find the family, read it plainly, start where the person is, say what it asks, answer in their own mirror, talk in its room, follow its threads, what differs by kind. About 4,200 characters, under the 7,000 a small model holds. Under the shared project lock.
- `function:document` now closes `How a mind helps its person read one and answer it is ways:reader.` Its text before stands at `archive:function:document:2026-11-15`, and its 9 says so. So every proposal, inquiry and plain document is handed the reader's way, `function:proposal` naming no way (its `ways:proposal` is for whoever makes one).

**The ladder says an address as it is answered at.** On a family off the clock, `pscale_stream_engage` printed each rung of its ladder as bare digits (`[1118]`) while its header and readings said `1.118`. Both fresh readers in the proof below stumbled on it and could have answered at the wrong place. `ladderLines` now shows each rung with its one decimal point at the floor; a clock family keeps its full-width beat.

## The proof

`npm run smoke:way-by-kind`, offline against two in-memory beaches (a family's own and the default): 13 of 13. A proposal is handed the reader's way by its mode; a recipe the recipe's way, its phase before its mode; a law naming its own way keeps it; neither line, nothing; a phase with no form falls to the mode; a form missing at the family's beach is found at the default one and its way read there; the second engage in a session prints only the line saying the way was given, and asks the forms nothing; off the clock the ladder says 1.1 and 1.18 with their decimal point.

Two fresh minds were each given only the first message the site's new door writes, one for this proposal at its third step and one for the first recipe, with the open connector and no way to write. Each read the family, said what its place asks and whom it waits on, and gave the exact call that keeps the person's answer at the right address. Their stumbles mended what they read: the key a first write needs (`ways:reader` 5, `ways:recipe` 4), the recipe's help room and how it keeps the way's steps, and this ladder.
