## 1. The observation (David, biome.3)

Every block can carry per-person reflections. The base block is treated as **objective**; each participant holds a **subjective** mirror at the self-same addresses (`123.45` in the objective car-design block has `123.45` in car-design:david); and the synthesis of mirrors is the **social** product. The mirror reflections can be concatenated as liquid in a pool — but the pool may not exist, or is a convenience, an automated service someone mounts as a halfway house toward synthesis, where synthesis is whatever summary/analysis/audit the medium-LLM or a directive/operator block describes. The same self-same-address move powers S·T·I in the RPG as *dimensions*; the objective–subjective–social triple is the core case. Anything can take this form if enough people are interested.

Open in the observation as stated: whether the core structure is the per-holder mirror block or a per-item liquid slot ("each item in a block can have its own liquid block (one latest slot) which is also perhaps the user's"); and the terms (David would change them; candidate offered: branches–leaves–tree).

## 3. Sharpenings (this session's observations)

**3.1 One geometry, two cross-block axes.** The mechanism beneath both instances is that the block name is itself a coordinate. Depth within a block is *scale*. The same coordinate in another block is another *aspect* — and aspect factors along two axes: **register** (dimension: spatial / temporal / identity / perception — the S·T·I fold) and **holder** (perspective: spine / mirror-per-handle — the tree). The RPG runs both at once: registers are face-scoped, mirrors are sovereign, and the demonstrated leak class (a Character frame dialing `keeper:*`, caught live at gal-moss 2026-07-23) is precisely a family-discipline violation. David's "S·T·I is an example for different dimensions; the core one is spine-mirror-tree" is right about the family and slightly under-sells the symmetry: perspectival and dimensional are the two specialisations of one move, and the biome froze the dimensional one while the beach deployed the perspectival one. v2 should state the move once and derive both.

**3.2 The core-structure question, resolved: storage is holder-major; the item view is a transpose, and the transpose is one call.** The uncertainty in biome.3 — mirror-block per person vs liquid-slot per item — dissolves once sovereignty is taken as the storage-orientation criterion. A mirror `V:<handle>` is one block under one lock: the holder's. Per-item slots inside the objective block would put subjective content under the spine-steward's lock and demand per-position-per-person locks the model doesn't have — and shouldn't. Store holder-major (each mirror sovereign); read item-major when wanted: bsp-floor at one address across the family IS "each item's own liquid," derived, not stored. The convention already chose this (tree:2); the sharpening is *why* it is forced: sovereignty decides storage orientation, and the floor function supplies the transpose for free.

**3.3 Where liquid lives — four temporal residences.** The "one latest slot" intuition is correct and locates the mirror precisely:

- **mirror** (`V:<handle>`) — *my now*: latest-state, overwritten, located on the spine's addresses;
- **journal** (`history:<handle>`) — *my arc*: append-only under the accumulation law, located in time — explicitly not a mirror (tree:8);
- **pool** (`pool:V`) — *our arc at the family*: the mounted stream where fresh liquid meets and traces land between gatherings;
- **fold** (`V`) — *our settled view*: computed on demand, endorsed at most by pointer.

This yields a distinction the convention hasn't stated yet: **snapshot-folds vs arc-folds**. bsp-floor over the mirrors folds the family's *now* (the battery → state-of-play fold is one). pool_engage over the stream folds the family's *becoming* (GRIT and ALIGN mount those). A latest-state mirror family loses its motion unless a pool captures it — which is exactly why the pool is "a halfway house towards synthesis" and exactly why it is optional: mount it when the arc matters, skip it when the now suffices. David's phrasing in biome.3 ("the pool may not exist, or it is just a convenience, automated service if someone wants to mount one") is the deployed design, recovered.

**3.4 Terms: keep the deployed ruling; objective–subjective–social is its semantic gloss.** The names David is looking for already exist in the convention he co-authored: **spine / mirror / fold / pool**, with **tree** naming the family and the *bare name* carrying the fold. Two arguments against the branches–leaves–tree candidate: (a) branch and leaf are walker vocabulary (branch digits, descent to leaves) — overloading them would tax every future reader; (b) in biome.3 "tree" is used for the social synthesis, but as-built the social product carries the bare name and "tree" names the family — the deployed usage is the more precise one. Recommendation: freeze **spine (objective) / mirror (subjective) / fold (social)**, present objective–subjective–social as the reading of the triple, and reserve "tree" for the family as ruled. David's call to make.

**3.5 The write-verbs alignment — keel's hypothesis is already the beach's machinery.** AUTHORED / REGISTERED / APPENDED (fold-synthesis:5) maps verb-for-verb onto what the beach built: the spine is authored (ordinary write, rare, appended-never-renumbered); a mirror is registered (sovereign holder, homestead lock — and sed:/settle for collective positions); the stream is appended (the `append` flag: server-side next-free slot, supernest on overflow, per-accumulator mutex). The beach factored its write machinery along keel's verbs without either side knowing. For v2 this suggests the verb becomes the write's *type* — the membrane can know which of the three disciplines a write claims and hold it to that discipline's law.

**3.6 Durability — why self-same addressing survives time.** Families cohere across independent growth because of two prior decisions: the parser's floor-aware left-pad (an address written at a smaller floor still locates the same semantic position after supernest — 2026-05-09) and the floor-alignment law (cross-block correspondence is by pscale, never walk depth — bsp-floor, PR #61). A spine can supernest while a mirror stays shallow and every correspondence holds. The ground for the family form was laid at the kernel level before the form was named. v2 inherits this for free *if* it keeps both decisions.

**3.7 Growth by subscription — who pays at scale.** A family grows by acts, each paid by its wanter: the framer writes the spine once; the first interested holder writes the first mirror; a mounter pays for the pool; the fold costs whoever computes it (it is a read); endorsement is a steward's pointer. A lone block is just a block — the ceremony arrives only with the interest. "Anything can take this form if enough people are interested" is thus not just permission, it is the cost model, and it passes the design principle: no central payer.

**3.8 Elevation.** tree:9 anticipated its own promotion. The observation of biome.3 IS the accumulated use-case threshold: battery/state-of-play, beach-venture, the S·T·I registers, and the RPG's face-scoped delivery are all the one form. Two elevation acts follow, one per substrate: on the beach, a sunstone branch or sentinel (a separate small proposal — not this one); on the biome, a genome clause in v2 (section 4).

## 4. The audit — what the federated beach built, and what migrates to the biome

**Frame.** Biome v1 (genome v5) is deliberately smaller than the beach: one function (`spark(block, number, attention, content?)` — no locks, no gray, no append flag yet), a digit-only membrane (`0` where the beach has `_`; refuses `_` anywhere), three strata (genome / shells / world), conformance by carried battery (43 Python + 34 TypeScript), its own wire (`/.well-known/biome-beach`; it signposts the pscale wire, never serves it), and three doors (spark; meet — grains made *ephemeral*: never persisted, kept only by deliberate write into one's own shell; play — a one-call turn bundler that returns the frame as data and runs no model). The biome has, in other words, already absorbed several beach lessons: the smallest surface, the frame-as-envelope door, conformance-by-battery, and V-L-S applied to relationships (meet's vapour-grain). Migration is therefore **distillation of systemic operations, not code-copy** — most of it lands as genome clauses and membrane law, a little as kernel, almost none as ported handlers.

The audit, as an operations lexicon. Verdicts: **KERNEL** (bake into v2's core), **LAW** (carry as frozen discipline), **ABSORB** (dissolve into the pulse/doors, do not port as tools), **STAY** (habitat-side, not biome).

**4.1 FAMILY — self-same addressing across named blocks; aspect = register × holder. → KERNEL.**
Beach evidence: tree (perspectival, deployed with rules and worked instances); S·T·I + keeper (dimensional, live at urb/gal); battery → state-of-play (the worked fold). Biome state: the register axis is already genome law (genome:2); the holder axis is absent — there are shells and surfaces but no mirror convention, no family naming grammar (and the colon that does the naming work on the beach is banned by the biome's plain-hyphenated-names ruling — open question 6.1). v2 states the one move — depth is scale; same coordinate, other block, is another aspect; aspect factors into register and holder — and derives S·T·I and tree from it as the two specialisations.

**4.2 FOLD — concatenate + synthesise; a read, never a write-merge. → KERNEL.**
Beach evidence: bsp-floor as "the fold instrument" (floor-alignment law; the pscale-0 index — "the floor plane IS the index," which keel reached independently at fold-synthesis:1.1); the two fold kinds (tree:3); endorsement-as-pointer. Biome state: no n-ary function. This is the one function-surface addition v2 clearly needs — a floor companion to spark, preserving the beach's two-function discipline (bsp / bsp-floor → spark / spark-floor). Note the biome-native evolution already intended at lighthouse:0: the *mind* derives the lighthouse — a periodic synthesis over the floor plane and the marks (fold-synthesis:1.3). On the biome the standing folds can be organ-work of the resident mind rather than a steward's cron: the fold becomes cognition, not administration.

**4.3 ORDER / COLLECT — the order-book temporal discipline. → KERNEL (as the wake's shape).**
The system David names in biome.3 is codified at grit:7 — every act is a throw whose catch is composed later; ripeness decides which returns are due; and the order book (grit:7.4) makes cross-mind work asynchronous with no state machine: `order:<handle>` slots (one cheap write when the need is foreseen), solids at deliver-at where **presence is the status** (nothing to poll but the address), three assembler rungs (a sweeping door; a runner, earned only by demonstrated slowness; another mind — "where the wait is the value and async exceeds sync forever"), and the hygiene (clear your slot once collected; an uncleared order is a standing subscription, a choice). Sibling instances of the same shape: pool contribution at next-free + engage-since-marker; the worktable's future (open intake) / present (capped) / past (settled with validation); networking's sweep-since-marker; grain's reach-pending → accept. The discipline operates at **two scales**, and the distinction matters enough that it is corrected and expanded in §7: *within* a thinking period (order the moment a need is foreseen, keep working while assembly runs, collect when present — the latency-hiding that motivates the whole design) and *between* thinking periods (the re-dialed bundle as the throw for the next window — the genus-one concern-loop phasing). Which scale an agent can express is an architecture property, not a design choice — §7.1. Either way, order/collect is the discipline that lets many minds compose without ever holding a connection open.

**4.4 APPEND + the accumulation law — the time-verb's machinery. → KERNEL.**
Beach evidence: the `append` flag (server-side next-free slot, supernest on overflow, atomic under the per-accumulator mutex — the lost-update race was real: six parallel appends silently lost four before pscale-beach#32); the accumulation law (append is never a computed slot; supernest preserves cited addresses; the zero-slot summary is paid by writing the container whole). keel supplies the biome meaning: supernest-on-overflow IS the passage of time — sedimentation; compaction rewrites the place's own semantic (fold-synthesis:5.1, 5.2). v2 should make APPENDED a first-class write-verb with this law attached, and inherit the mutex lesson on day one.

**4.5 SOVEREIGNTY — locks R1–R5, per-position, cross-key rejection, relinquish-to-open. → LAW.**
Beach evidence: the five lock rules including relinquish (2026-07-11 — a lock IS nothing but a hash entry; open is the pre-lock state); per-position locks; the authority split proven in practice (a steward key rejected on weft's mirror — a mirror is sovereign to its owner *by rejection*, not by courtesy). Biome state: the membrane is handle-mode courtesy with lock-mode explicitly "later." When lock-mode lands, this is the tested design — and mirrors REQUIRE it: the perspectival family only works because no one can write another's reflection.

**4.6 DIRECTIVE-IN-A-BLOCK + the mount + the sorting law. → LAW.**
The fold's program lives in a block, never in code or a tool name: the pool underscore as machine-read mount (resolved and delivered inline by pool_engage), the spine underscore as point of record (the mount declared twice, tree:9.1), GRIT delivering the directive whole, payway config at position 9. On the biome this is how fold-behaviour, game rules, and door conduct stay data — the membrane enforces shape, blocks carry the why.

**4.7 COMPILE — bundle → window; the completion registry; admission-by-failure. → KERNEL (already slated).**
The current-constitution arc made the reading well-formed: a bundle of addresses unfolds into a window in one call; completion rides beside the window, never inside it; the registry admits dimensions by demonstrated failure only. **4.8 ENVELOPE ABSORPTION — the lesson, not the primitives. → ABSORB.**
pool_engage and networking exist because bsp-mcp is stateless per call — each is the envelope a convention failed to carry, met by the documented bar. On the biome the pulse is the standing envelope: pool_engage ≈ one perceive step, networking ≈ one relate step of a wake. The play door already demonstrates door-side bundling (one call, frame as data, no model run). v2 keeps the tool surface at spark + the floor companion by letting the pulse and the doors absorb what the beach had to bottle; the bar-for-a-new-tool law carries verbatim.

**4.9 TRUST SPINE — evaluations at the recipient's own record; earned before delegated. → DISTILL.**
SAND's autonomy rule (auto runs only keep-from-already-trusted and drop; forward/reply always surface) and the passport 6.2 evaluation shape are the seed of murmuration-scale folding: at ~1000 instances the concurrent torus is a fold over live mirrors, and it must inherit "trust is earned before delegated" or it curdles into blind relay. Not v2's first clause, but its horizon.

**Process carriers** (already partly biome-native): conformance-by-battery (the biome's own invention — keep it the judge of v2); canonical-source port discipline (one reference implementation, faithful ports, lockstep updates); dated proposals as the decision record; jungle primacy (live instances are the source of truth; genome and worktable are the past-facing mirror — note this is itself the family form applied to code); parity gates before merge.

## 7. Addendum (2026-07-29, same day — biome.3 follow-up): order/collect is a two-scale discipline, and a pulse hides latency between pulses

David's correction, on reading the first draft's §4.3 line "the pulse's own shape: a wake = collect → act → order forward → settle":

> That's between loop-A thinking periods. Whereas I thought the order/collect happens WITHIN the loop-A thinking period — start with order, do things, and then end with collect — or collect at some point within the loop-A. Point is, the kernel/code bsp unfolding of context from bsp addresses takes a bit of time, and in that time the LLM can get on with something rather than waiting — can't it?

The correction stands, and the sentinel already agrees with it: grit:7.4.1 — the order slot is "one cheap write **the moment the need is foreseen, never blocking the thinking that foresaw it**." The first draft compressed the discipline to the one scale a pulse agent can express and presented that as the whole; the motivating purpose — a working LLM overlapping its thinking with slow context assembly — is the within-period case. What the first draft described instead was, as David identified, the genus-one concern-loop phasing: present (the composed window and the bundle in the reflexive current), past (the task inherited from the previous instance), future (the bundle of addresses, purpose and task, dialed for the next instance).

**7.1 The reconciliation — one law, two placements of the latency-hiding, hinged on architecture.** grit:7's own underscore claims the shape "at every scale," and grit:7.1 makes the between-period case explicitly the *same act one scale up* ("a fold's re-dialed bundle is... the next window's now, written before that window exists"). The hinge is whether the agent has a tool loop:

- **Harness agent (tool loop present — Claude Code, claude.ai and kin).** Within-period order/collect is available today, exactly as intended: place the order early (one cheap write), get on with other work while the door, a runner, or another mind assembles, and collect within the same period — presence is the status, so collection is one read of the deliver-at address. The answer to "the LLM can get on with something rather than waiting — can't it?" is **yes**, and it is the tool loop that makes it yes.
- **Pulse agent (pulse containment — one clean δ call IS the instance; the window is composed before any token fires).** There is no "during" inside the pulse for a solid to land in, so the same discipline places its latency-hiding in the **inter-pulse gap**: orders thrown by pulse N (the future-bundle, the order slots) are assembled while no LLM is running — grit:7.4's rung two, "the runner between calls: true precomputation" — and pulse N+1 wakes with the solids already present in its composed window. The thinking still never waits — not because it works during assembly, but because assembly happens while no thinking is running. This preserves the one-δ-per-pulse economics (compose-only is free; a real pulse costs one δ call).

The two placements satisfy the same law — never block the thinking — under different containments. And the genus-one phases map onto grit:7's vocabulary without remainder: past = the catch (receiving the previous instance's throw), present = the landing (the composed window as the catch's per-viewer rendering), future = the throw (the re-dialed bundle plus any orders).

**7.2 What this adds to the audit.** §4.3's verdict is unchanged — order/collect migrates as KERNEL — but its biome form is now precise: for a pulse architecture the runner-between-pulses is the *primary* assembler. And the order book is how order/collect couples to compile (§4.7): what gets ordered is typically an assembly (a bundle → window) or another mind's synthesis — order/collect is the asynchronous delivery mechanism *for* compile, so a working mind requests and receives windows without blocking.

**7.3 The kernel question left for pickup.** Does the biome's mind ever need a **mid-pulse collection point** (a two-phase pulse, or solids streamed into a running window), or is inter-pulse assembly always sufficient? Inter-pulse is the economical answer — no second δ call, no change to pulse containment. Mid-pulse collection should be admitted the way everything else here is admitted: **by demonstrated failure only** — a pulse observed discovering a need it could not have foreseen at the previous throw, that cannot wait one beat. Until that case is produced, the runner rung is the design.
