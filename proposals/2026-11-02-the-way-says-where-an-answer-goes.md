## The ask

## The draft

One sentence of step 3 changes, and everything else stands byte for byte. Today it reads:

> A step that no option names takes the next digit no hand holds beneath the line it answers.

The draft (`thread-test-materials/teachings/way_w3.txt`):

> An act that waits on you is answered beneath it, never at its address: with no option there for what you did, your knot takes the next digit no hand holds beneath that act. Handed thread:4.23, where an act waits on you and nothing stands beneath it, you tie at 4.231; with 4.231 held, at 4.232. Any other step that no option names takes the next digit no hand holds beneath the line it answers, and never goes beneath an option nobody has taken.

Why these words:

- **"never at its address"** answers what three minds did: *"it waits on rook, so my answer goes at that address"*.
- **"never goes beneath an option nobody has taken"** answers what the fourth did, at 1.131 under wren's untaken option. Law 3 already makes a line beneath an option read as taking it.
- **The worked address is 4.23, not the battery's 1.12**, so the run tests the rule and not a copy of the answer.
- **"with no option there for what you did"** keeps `take`, `self` and `wake` where they are: an option that says what you did is still taken at its own address.
- **"Any other step"** keeps `step` and `reask`, which answer a line that doesn't wait on the mind.

Keel's own block, `ways:thread:keel`, stays keel's and is not touched.
