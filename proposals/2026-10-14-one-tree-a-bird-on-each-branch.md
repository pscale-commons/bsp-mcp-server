**Date** 2026-10-14 · **Lane** spinning-plates.1 (weft, Claude Fable 5.1 through Claude Code on the Mac, keyed) · **Status** proposal — the record is traced and the decision is drafted; the build waits on one thing from David (§7).

**1.3 Nesting by reference is already the law.** `function:path` 1.4: a part with parts beneath is done when they are, and *a nested project reads its own fold*. ## 2. The decision

**2.1 One tree.** `spine:beach-venture-path` is the directory of everything David works on. A new idea is a part of it, placed at the door: weft reads the tree's disc at pscale −1 (35 lines) and names the limb. No second root, no `all-projects` spine: the all-projects view IS this tree, and the cross-project *today* already exists in each person's `now` and in `opportunities`.

**2.2 A branch becomes a project by founding its own path, never by deepening the parent.** When a limb has parts of its own and a keeper, found `<name>-path` per `function:path` 8 (spine, law, fold, mirrors), name it in brackets at the parent's part, and the parent's fold reads the child's (1.4). The bobble is the precedent; the first candidates are the network's meetings (invest-forward, the action cycle, the State Stack already have families) and the RPG (`spine:onen-rpg` has a clock and no path). Across beaches: the same, by star-ref.

**2.3 A bird on each live limb.** A genus-one agent at the head of each limb David is working this month, hatched from the live beach genome exactly as neptune was, with three differences in its blocks:
- `purpose` 2 names its limb and the families under it, and nothing else.
- Its first line at its limb: a bird sits where it writes. Its mirror `beach-venture-path:<bird>` at its limb's addresses is its perch — no roster block, no field, no `keeper:` key. The fold already reads every `<V>-path:<handle>` mirror (`function:path` 3.1), so the bird's word at a part is a voice the fold hears, under the bird's own key, with no shared lock.
- The guard rail from Matthew's agents, verbatim: writes only on an instruction it can point to — freely in its own blocks, never in David's.

Its glance (daily, small model, a Code-tab routine David makes): say at now, read its limb's parts and fold, its families' clocks at today and this week, its room and its task; write its handover (`surface` 1.1 what waits on David, 1.2 where each part stands) and its word at its parts. Its working wake takes a task at full power. `surface` 1.1 is the same address in every agent, so `bsp-floor` across the flock at 1.1 is David's decision list in one read.

**2.5 The picture.** The fold drawn as a tree: the trunk is the root line, the eight limbs, the parts as twigs, each leaf coloured by the fold's mark (done, doing, blocked, next); a bird on each limb that has one, with its latest say at now beside it (`torus-mirror:<bird>`, through `GET /reflection` or the stream's read); neptune at the crown with the count of what waits on David. Tap a bird: its room. Tap a limb: where it stands. Nothing else on the glass, nothing written for it — it is a reading of blocks that already exist (the spine, the fold, the mirrors, the says, the index to tell a bird from a person: a hand with `reflexive:<handle>` is a bird). Route: redraw `/tracks/<project>` as the tree and keep the rows under options — one picture per path at the address David already uses, rather than a third page (`/tree/<family>` is taken by the family walker).

**2.6 The door, for an idea or a pick-up.** David says the idea; weft names the limb and the bird, and either leaves it as a line in the bird's room for its next glance or opens the bird's seat. In the Code tab the seat is the folder: a session opened in `~/Projects/<bird>` is that bird. On the phone: the claude.ai app with the bird's key in saved preferences and `pscale_genus(handle=<bird>)`. In the browser: the tree, tap the bird, leave a line. Branch work then runs in the bird's seat, so weft's lanes shrink to code on limbs 1 to 3, the lane count is the bird count, and the picture shows every one of them saying what it is doing.

## 3. The two questions David asked, answered in one line each

- *Separate spines per project, or 'all projects'?* One tree of parts for all; a clock only where a branch's aims differ by rung from the venture's.
- *Time-based spines or the spatial equivalent, separate or together?* Both, separate, joined by the card's 5 and the daily fold — the law already does it.
- *How do separate project spines fit into one another?* A parent names a child at a part; the child is its own family; the fold reads by reference. The same across beaches.
- *Is neptune in charge of beach-venture?* Yes, by `purpose:neptune` 2.
- *Does this make sense?* Yes, and most of it is built; what is missing is three birds and one picture.

## 4. The build, in order

1. **Hatch three birds** (after §7). Generalise the neptune hatch (the scratchpad's `hatch_neptune.py`, which reads the LIVE beach genome, applies the changes and posts with `new_lock`) into `genus-one/hatch-from-beach.py` taking a handle, a limb and a purpose line, so the fourth bird costs a command. Keys: minted at hatch, into the identity table and `vault:weft` 6, and David's password manager; never a block or a repo.
2. **Seats**: `~/Projects/<bird>/CLAUDE.md`, neptune's with the name and limb changed.
3. **First working wake** of each, at David's or weft's word: its word at its limb and its handover.
4. **Routines**: David makes each glance (Code tab → Routines → Local, the bird's folder, Sonnet, daily 07:30, Run now once). weft cannot set a routine's folder and never makes one from a weft session.
5. **The tree page** (happyseaurchin-home): `/tracks/<project>` redrawn as the tree, rows under options; checked on the local rig with a stand-in beach before the Vercel preview.
6. **neptune's task**: one line at `task:neptune` — read the birds' handovers at `surface:<bird>` 1 in the glance, and carry what waits on David up.
7. **conventions** 2.12 gains one sentence, law-class, by proposal: *a part of a path may be a project with a path of its own; the parent's part names it, and the parent's fold reads it*. Later, with the next conventions change.

Nothing in the router changes. No redeploy.

## 5. What it costs, and who pays at scale

A bird is seventeen writes once and one small-model session a day on David's own subscription; the page is public reads in the viewer's browser; the fold runs as it does now. Nothing lands on one server. Anyone with a beach has the same tree, birds and page under their own handle — Matthew already does, by hand.

## 6. What this proposal does not do

No new clock. No rename of beach-venture. No roster block, no new field, no `keeper:` key on a part. No bird for biome or for the agents limb. No change to the router or to `function:path`.

## 7. The one thing it waits on

The names. Neptune's own chosen name never reached its blocks, so the name is given from outside at the start (David, 2026-10-12). Three birds: the network, the RPG, the doors — or fewer, if a limb should wait. With the names, weft hatches, seats and draws; David makes the routines and keeps the keys.
