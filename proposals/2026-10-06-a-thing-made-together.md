## 0. The answer in one paragraph

The library gains a sixth form, **a thing made together** (`function:make`), drawn from the law the bobble
already keeps (`function:bobble`). It holds what is particular to making a real thing with the people who want
it:
- the doors such a thing usually has;
- the road's shape: channels that run at once, ladders where the order is locked, and a gate where they meet;
- two readings the crowd gives, WANT and OFFER;
- money that moves only as sharing, through a ticket.

The generic parts are left to the path. How parts are written, the marks, the daily fold, who runs it and how
it is drawn all belong to `function:path`, the parallel lane's law. The form names them by address and never
restates them. /found offers the form and seeds its seven usual doors. The bobble mounts it as its first
instance.

## 1. What stands

- **The library.** `tree` 9.2 names five forms and "your own form": the venture, a set of questions, a
  progression, a health board and a neighbourhood of gifts. /found reads each form's founding branch at its
  operator's 8. A form can seed parts only if it is a set of questions (its seeds stand at the operator's 7).
  Every other form starts the founder on one empty part.
- **The bobble on a form of its own.** `function:bobble` 1 reads *"FORM — own, no library operator mounted"*.
  Its 2–7 are general to anything made together:
  - mirrors at the spine's addresses;
  - the fold at the bare name;
  - the room and the tickets;
  - silence and growth;
  - who keeps which branch;
  - what a person leaves readable.

  They were written for one device, but nothing in them is about the device.
- **The path, merged yesterday** (#489). Every project gets its parts beside its clock, the marks DONE, DOING,
  BLOCKED and NEXT, and a daily fold from the clock onto the parts. Two of its lines are this form's
  invitation:
  - *"A project founded as parts is already its own path. The bobble's spine is its parts and its mirrors are
    the readings, so nothing moves."*
  - *"A form sets its default. A thing made together writes its road as ladders."*

## 2. Who keeps what: the path and the form

| | `function:path` (the parallel lane) | `function:make` (this) |
|---|---|---|
| a part | written in the tree 9.3 grammar; spreads by scale; room at every node | the doors a thing made together usually has, seeded at founding |
| order | locked or loose: the shape is the lock; a form sets its default | the default: channels side by side, ladders where order is locked, a gate (*Done when*, *Now*, *Opens when*) |
| a hand's reading | DONE, DOING, BLOCKED | adds WANT (once per person, at the door for having one: the crowd's count) and OFFER (a skill, a part, a workshop, time, a gift) |
| the fold | the daily fold: one line for each part that moved, NEXT where a part opens | written at the bare name, because the thing is its own path, where /walk already reads it (happyseaurchin-home #380); its root carries three counts: how many want one, the offers at each door, and which of the gate's conditions stand |
| money | none | sharing only: gifts, pledges held against a goal, and tickets through the family's ticket collective (payway); credits are minted only at that boundary |
| who runs it | anyone holding the project's lock, with the law delivered whole | unchanged |

The form cites the path's branches (`function:path` 2, 3 and 4). If the path's law moves, the form follows
without an edit, and nothing in it overrides the path.

## 3. The form, branch by branch

The full text stands at `function:make` on the beach, in the tree 9.3 grammar.

- **0 MAKE.** Read the family as a thing being made by the people who want one. It is already its own path.
  Mount it as `form: function:make` at a family law's 1.
- **1 THE DOORS.** The usual doors are:
  - the whole thing, general to specific, as one walk down its first branch;
  - I want one;
  - I want to build one;
  - making and carrying them;
  - how the money works;
  - can it be done;
  - where it stands and the road.

  A family keeps its own doors and grows by depth.
- **2 THE ROAD.** Channels run at once, ladders where the order is locked, and a gate where they meet.
  Out-of-order work is marked as it happened.
- **3 THE MIRRORS.** WANT, OFFER, DONE, DOING and BLOCKED come first, then the hand's own words.
- **4 THE FOLD.** The path's daily fold, at the bare name. Every line opens with its mark, so /walk draws it under its part, and the three counts ride the fold's root line.
- **5 THE ROOM AND THE TICKETS.** Sharing words only: share, give, pledge, back, carry, and never buy, sell or
  invest.
- **6 WHO KEEPS WHAT.** The keeper adopts. A branch can be handed to a steward under their own key. What the
  thing hears stays its owner's.
- **7 SILENCE AND GROWTH.** An unvoiced address is not yet spoken. A new direction begins in a mirror.
- **8 FOUNDING.** The spine, the law and the room, each with its birth line at its digit's 1.
- **9 PROVENANCE.**

## 4. Founding, and the seeds

- **Where the seeds stand.** The seven usual doors stand beneath the spine's birth line, at `function:make`
  8.1's 2. /found reads them (happyseaurchin-home #381, merged) and seeds the parts the founder edits, keeps
  or removes.
- **No other form changes.** A form with no seeds still starts with one empty part. A set of questions still
  seeds from its 7.
- **Why seven, not the bobble's eight.** A node holds five to seven parts, so the next has room (`function:path`
  1). Making and carrying start as one door. A run that grows big enough gives it two parts beneath, by depth
  rather than breadth.
- **What a founding writes.** /found writes these blocks:
  - the spine at floor 3, opening with the founder's sentence;
  - the law, with `form: function:make` at its 1;
  - the room, whose opening line speaks to whoever arrives (conventions 2.14).

  The mirrors and the fold are born of use.

## 5. The bobble, its first instance

- **The mount.** `function:bobble` 1 is re-voiced to open `form: function:make`. It keeps the bobble's own
  reading of its road (four channels, one gate, the make's four conditions), and its tokens become the form's.
  The standing text is first copied to `archive:function:bobble:2026-10-06`.
- **The rest of its law stands.** Its 2–7 are where the form came from, and they say the same in the bobble's
  words.
- **Its spine keeps eight doors and the law's pointer.** It came before the room rule, and its doors are its
  own.

## 6. What it does not do

- **No new primitive and no new field.** One small /found change seeds a form's parts. /walk is untouched:
  the row grammar already draws the doors and ladders, and a reading that opens in capitals is already counted,
  WANT and OFFER included.
- **It does not touch the path.** `function:path`, `spine:beach-venture-path`, /path and the evening fold belong
  to the parallel lane.
- **No clock.** A thing made together that wants one adds the clock family, and its law names the parts, as the
  path says.

## 7. What waits on David

- **The name `function:make`**, short like its neighbours (backcast, respond, audit, status, offers). The
  bobble's gate is also called "the make". They mean the same thing, making it, but if the echo jars,
  `function:craft` is the next choice.
- **WANT is a signal, not a promise.** It counts the crowd without asking anything of them. A pledge stays a
  ticket.
