# Transpose to depth, and the honed bundle — the extraction check becomes law, and every call's spindles earn their words

**Status**: PROPOSED (ruled "core" by David 2026-08-12 in the xstream.11 arc; this file is the
proposal-first half of the law-writes discipline — the block amendments happen only after this
merges, each with its `archive:<block>:<date>` version cut first).

**Two rulings, one root.** Both halves of this proposal are the same discipline applied at two
scales: *words in a block earn their position by what a walk of them delivers* (the transpose
law, per-block), and *words in a response envelope earn their place by what the calling mind
does next* (the honed bundle, per-call). Selection, not accretion, in both.

---

## 1 — The demonstrated fault

2026-08-12: the `mirror` charter's first authoring (weft) was a flat fan — four branches of
same-bracket categories, spindles two deep, long underscores doing heading-work. The keeper's
critique, verbatim in effect: *the intro in underscore could be shorter and cleaner; each item
is a dimension which can fan, and can be grown well beyond 9 nestings if need be; the
evaluation test is in the spindle extraction — and two-deep spindles just aren't that useful.
The LLM doesn't compare, it just creates its block and assumes the spindles are useful.*

This is not a one-off. It is the documented core authoring fault of the resident instance
(flat same-bracket categorisation), it recurs under time pressure because fanning is cheap and
depth requires knowing the content's grain, and the existing law already forbids it —
sunstone:8.5 ("author depth, not breadth; design by concept, which nests, never by category,
which fans flat") is even carried in the bsp() tool description. **The gap is not the rule.
The gap is the check.** Nothing at write-time makes the author *walk what they wrote*.

The rewrite that answered the critique passed its own test: the charter's deepest spindle
(`mirror:1.21`) now delivers *the test → the journey → exactly what is missing* in three
self-contextualising rungs. That walk is the shape of the check this proposal makes law.

## 2 — The law: TRANSPOSE, then EXTRACT

Two clauses, to be added where §3 names.

**T (transpose to depth first).** Before writing, transpose the material to depth: the
primary line of each dimension DESCENDS — each rung a tighter refinement of its parent — and
a fan appears only where items are genuinely parallel *at that rung*. A fan of categories at
pscale 0 with two-deep stubs beneath is the failure form (strata already names it: the fan).
Depth is unbounded; a dimension may run well past nine rungs by nesting. Categories, where
they exist at all, stand at one nest level inside a dimension — never as the block's shape.

**X (the extraction check — the read-back law, sharpened).** After the write, walk ONE
spindle — the deepest, or the most load-bearing — exactly as its future reader will receive
it (the path-walk: every ancestor underscore riding above the terminus). Judge the walk, not
the tree: **if any rung fails to self-contextualise the next, or the walk ends before it has
taught anything a reader could act on, the block has failed the check — transpose and rewrite
before calling the write done.** A spindle of depth two is presumptively failing: it had no
room to teach. Where the author genuinely cannot tell, write the fan AND the transposed form,
extract from both, and keep the better — the comparison the keeper observed LLMs never make,
made mechanical.

X extends, and does not replace, the standing read-back law (well-formed battery, and the
closing step of strata's writing spindle): the read-back said *walk it back*; X says *and
judge the walk by what it delivers*.

## 4 — The honed bundle: every call's spindles earn their words

**The vision (David, 2026-08-12):** every tool response carries a small bundle of spindles —
tight, operational — and the bundle is HONED: entries begin big and fat, end lean and taut
but operationally essential; every change that is followed by an error rolls back; what
remains is calibrated to the capacity of the LLM making the call. *Models improve their own
self-selecting language patterns that work.*

**What already stands (the photo, 2026-08-12):**

- **The `now` stamp** (`src/temporal.ts`) rides EVERY bsp-mcp tool response — the first and
  so far only standing member of the bundle: one line, temporal address plus voiced beat.
- **The completion registry** (`src/compile.ts`) rides every compiled window — and it is
  already selection-shaped: it **admits by failure only** (one entry: RELATION, from the
  2026-07-21 sovereignty overstep; GROUND waits for a demonstrated failure). Entries are
  scooped LIVE from their blocks, so improving the block improves every future footer.
- **The stance blocks** (`grips`, `reflective-compass`) ride composed windows as stance.
- **`lodestone`** (sentinel) maps WHAT a constituted instance answers to — six dimensions
  plus the window-act — and already carries the size law at its 7.4: *the set has to stay
  small, because a window holding everything important holds nothing salient.*
- **`reflective-compass`** (genome organ, mirrored per instance) is the capacity calibrator
  in embryo: its PARTICIPANT rung states *what reads this, and how far its spindle runs* — a
  bare model lands shallow, an agent runs to the floor. But the per-instance mirrors
  (`reflective-compass:egg-one` et al.) are today unhoned genome copies: **the honing loop
  does not exist yet.** That loop is what this section proposes.

**The honing law (proposed as convention, not code):**

- **Membership by failure.** A spindle enters the bundle only when its absence has produced a
  demonstrated error — the completion registry's standing rule, generalised. No entry ships
  on anticipation.
- **Wording by selection.** An entry's text may be leaned at any time BY ITS HOLDER (the
  instance whose mirror it is, or the sentinel's keeper), each change dated. **When an error
  follows a change, the change rolls back** — the previous wording is restored from the
  archive/git cut, and the failed lean is recorded beside it. Fat → lean is earned, one
  surviving change at a time.
- **Capacity calibration by depth, not by variant texts.** One spindle, written so that a
  shallower mind reads fewer rungs and a deeper mind walks further — the compass's
  how-far-the-spindle-runs pattern. The bundle never forks per model; the DEPTH READ is the
  calibration.
- **Delivery, not injection.** The bundle rides response envelopes (footer position, sibling
  to the `now` stamp), scooped live from blocks — never hard-coded text. Improving the block
  improves every subsequent call, and rolling back is a block write.

**Gate.** This is envelope work, and the bar for envelope machinery is the standing one: *the
envelope is observably what's missing, and conventions have failed to carry it.* The `now`
stamp and the completion registry already clear that bar for their entries. Further members —
the extraction check as a standing spindle, a compass line — enter one at a time through the
membership-by-failure rule, not as a batch. **No new tool; the surface stays twelve.** Phase
one of the honing loop is purely substrate-side: date-stamped leans and rollbacks on the
existing stance/registry blocks, driven by their holders.

## 5 — What this proposal deliberately does not do

- No transpose *tool* — transposition is authoring practice and check, not surface.
- No enforcement in the walker or the beach — a shape gate cannot judge whether a walk
  teaches; only a reader can. The law binds through the batteries and the read-back
  discipline, where the other authoring laws already live.
- No bundle expansion in this proposal — it establishes the honing law and names the standing
  members; each future member arrives by its own demonstrated failure.

## 6 — Adoption

Merge = the law is adopted. The three amendments in §3 land as their own PRs/writes, each
version-cut first. The honing law in §4 stands as convention from merge, exercised on the
blocks that already ride calls; its first test is the next demonstrated failure.
