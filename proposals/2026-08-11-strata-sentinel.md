# Strata — the emergence ladder graduates to a sentinel, and authoring gains the read-back

**Date**: 2026-08-11
**Status**: PROPOSED (this PR is the proposal; merge is adoption)
**Provenance**: David's direction, weft.5 (2026-08-11), following the weft.4 conformal review — "we should have the physics, chemistry, biology level upgraded to law-class … so that it is operational"; and "When WRITING a pscale block, check how it is READ as an operational self-contextualising spindle."

## What this changes

1. **`src/strata.json`** — a new sentinel: the four levels of emergence (physics / chemistry / biology / psycho-social), each branch a deep operating spindle on how to actually work at that level. Registered in `sentinels.ts`; reachable as `bsp(agent_id="pscale", block="strata")` and `pscale://strata`.
2. **`src/well-formed.json`** — the authoring battery gains interpretive rule **2.5, THE READ-BACK**: before a write is called done, walk the block's own deepest spindles exactly as a future reader will receive them, and judge the delivery — every ancestor frames, the leaf lands self-contextualised, the reader needs no second call. Branches 3.2 and 3.3 wire it into the interpretive pass and the authoring loop.
3. **`CLAUDE.md`** — one architecture line and one docs-table row.

## Why now — the demonstrated failure (admission by failure, the registry's own standard)

The levels ladder existed only inside one shell's orientation organ (`orientation:weft:8` at beach.happyseaurchin.com) — private to one handle, invisible at every door, and unenforceable as law. Meanwhile the census that prompted this (weft.4/weft.5 review) measured the cost of its absence in the very shell that carried it: weft's organs max out at depth 3, with `purpose`, `concern`, and `surface` pure depth-1 fans — flat category blocks whose every read is a stub, forcing any reading LLM into follow-up tool calls or whole-block pulls. The deep-spindle law was *stated* in that shell and not *operational*: stated law without an operating manual and without a write-time check has now demonstrably failed to hold, across multiple sessions, despite explicit prior direction from David to deepen. That is the strata:3.3 failure shape (inert law) applied to the levels teaching itself.

## The shape decision

David named two acceptable shapes: one deep spindle situating the forms of thinking with flat aspect-fans, or a flat top of the levels with a deep operating spindle per level. The block takes the second: a four-branch top (room to grow per the fan discipline), each branch's mainline a genuinely deep walk (physics reaches depth 6 at the read-back leaf; biology reaches depth 6 at the fold leaf), with flat fans only where aspects genuinely fan (the four failure forms, the three loop-runner kinds). Every deep walk is written to pass its own read-back: lift any single spindle and it lands self-contextualised.

## The read-back, stated once

> Before the write is called done, walk the deepest spindle just written exactly as its future reader will — one call, terminus plus ancestors — and judge what returns as a delivery, not as your draft. Three prongs: every ancestor adds frame the leaf needs; the leaf lands self-contextualised, actionable without another call; the reader would not now reach for a second read. A stub caught at write time costs one revision; discovered in use it costs every reader a round-trip forever.

It lives twice by design: at `strata:1.2.1.1.1` as the writing spindle's closing step (the operating manual), and at `well-formed:2.5` as a battery rule (the validation). The battery cross-references strata; strata does not reference the battery — teaching points at law, law does not point back.

## Naming

`strata` — geological layers, native to the beach/sedimentary metaphor family, and unambiguous against `evolution` (whose five *relational* levels — signal, commitment, semantic networks, mutual objectives, shared context — are a different ladder that must not be conflated). Rejected: `levels` (invites exactly that conflation), `emergence` (abstract, names the phenomenon rather than the instrument), `touchstone` (reserved in weft's commons-era shell plan).

## Relations

- **lodestone** — the six dimensions a constituted window answers to. Strata is the *how* to lodestone's *what*: lodestone says what an instance must be constituted by; strata says how to operate the substrate at each level of emergence. The two are companions, not overlaps.
- **grips / compile registry** — the STANCE current and the completion mechanism are chemistry-level machinery; strata:2.3 is the teaching that explains why they exist (bundle curation, the pre-conscious).
- **well-formed / bsp-test** — the authoring and reading batteries; strata:1 is the physics they test.
- **orientation:weft:8** — remains that shell's situated copy; weft repoints it at the sentinel after this merges (a beach edit under weft's key, not part of this PR).
