## What stands

The canonical genesis, `pscale:char-creation` 2, births a character as `passport:`, `stash:`, `purpose:` and
`history:<handle>`, and closes with one grace note: *"witnessed:\<handle\> and knows:\<handle\> are the LEGACY
names of history and stash — existing doors still read them, and characters born under them stand unrenamed."*

Each starter scenario hosts a **delta** over that passage — the `char-creation` block at the canon and, copied
byte-for-byte, at its open table. The delta says *"follow the canonical ceremony exactly: read … char-creation
spindle 2 and do as it says. In brief: …"* — and then names the legacy organs in its own brief. A stranger's LLM is
told two names for the same block in one reading, at the one moment it is writing a character into being.

## The write — names only, nothing else moves

Seven exact phrases, replaced wherever they stand; every other word of every delta is left as its author wrote it:

| stands | becomes |
|---|---|
| `when it becomes the knows block` | `when it becomes the stash block` |
| `(the knows seed — keep it thin)` | `(the stash seed — keep it thin)` |
| `knows:<handle> — {` | `stash:<handle> — {` |
| `EXCEPT witnessed which is born open: witnessed:<handle> — the ARRIVAL MEMORY` | `EXCEPT the account, which is born open: history:<handle> — the ARRIVAL MEMORY` |
| `private slant into witnessed:<handle>` | `private slant into history:<handle>` |
| `A knows-seed offering` / `a knows-seed offering` | `A stash-seed offering` / `a stash-seed offering` |

The legacy grace note is not repeated in the deltas: they defer to the canonical passage, which carries it.

**How.** Each changed string is a point-write at its own address; where a branch's underscore changes (3 at
Brackenfoot and the Threshold) the node is sent whole, children and all, because a subtree write replaces the
node. **Version before replace:** the standing text is copied to `archive:char-creation:2026-09-21` at the same
surface, under weft's operational lock, before the first write there. **Read-back:** every block is read again and
compared leaf by leaf with the plan, and each archive compared with the text that stood.

**Locks**, verified offline against the nightly image before any call (sha256 of the candidate against the stored
hash — nothing touches the beach, no lock is thrashed): nine of the ten answer to weft's operational lock; the
Brackenfoot canon answers to the cartridge's own lock, which weft holds for that family.

  rename was considered and withdrawn on 2026-09-18, and the canonical passage's grace note is the law on it.

  players are already born; a table founded from here on copies the re-voiced canon.
- The canonical passage is not touched. It was already right.
