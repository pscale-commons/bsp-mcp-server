## 1. What a person could not see

A hand was one block, `ahead:<handle>`, with a pile per rung, and each card named its project at field 2.
Nine was the size of a pile: one rung, across every project. Every page counts by project. /hands has a row
per project, and /walk counts every person's cards in one project. No page showed a pile. So on 12 October
the day's pile was full with seven cards toward now, one beach-venture and one wow-experiences. /hands said
8 and 5, /walk/beach-venture said 6 (David's 5 and one of weft's), and the nine appeared nowhere. A limit
only helps when the person can count it on screen.

## 2. The shape: a hand per project

- **`ahead:<project>:<handle>`** is the holder's hand in one project. It has the same seven rungs inside and
  nine cards to a rung. It is born latched under the holder's own key. A handle whose passport is locked
  founds it with the passport's key as the proof (pscale-beach, a handle founds under its own key), and the
  new hand may keep the key the holder's hands already use, so a person still has one key for their cards.
- **The project moves from a field into the name.** A card in a project hand carries no 2: the block it
  stands in names its project, as `tree:<field>:<handle>` names the field of a holder's syntheses.
- **now is the project every handle has**: `ahead:now:<handle>`, shown as "my now". A card written with no
  project is a now card.
- **Nine per rung per project is the structure's own nine.** A rung is one node of a project's hand, and a
  node has nine branches, so no count is kept anywhere. A person sees it as one project's cards at one rung.
- **The hand a person compiles is uncapped.** The cards held or being done, gathered at /hands and flicked at
  /next, stay a mark on each card (4: held, doing) across every project. Nothing limits how many.

Two other routes were weighed and not taken. Keeping one hand and counting per project in the pages would
need a rung to hold more than nine, so piles would have to grow, which the 1 September ruling settled
against. One level per project under each rung would need a fixed digit standing for each project's name,
and re-ordering a list would then move cards (a position is not a name).

## 3. Names a person reads

- **The rungs by their own names**: within hours · today · this week · this month · this season · this year ·
  this decade. The rung a person sees is the rung the card is in. This supersedes the 2026-09-14 label
  ('tomorrow' for 2, from *standing at rung R the card is the next R*). The digit for a rung is unchanged
  (it is the rung's pscale), so /walk's day row still mints into 2. /now's deal at the day moves from 1 to
  2 to match.
- **"aligned to"** replaces 'toward', and **"my now"** names the now project.
- **On ⇄** the rungs need no heading, because "today" and "this week" say themselves. The projects are
  headed "aligned to". A card reads `TODAY · aligned to beach-venture`.
- **/next's ↑** was "tomorrow": it threw a card into pile 2. It becomes **"later"**, one rung out: within
  hours → today → this week, and so on. The clock stops as before.
- **The bar's ＋ becomes a golden "＋card"**, gold being the cards' colour. Its card says "a card for today,
  aligned to my now · 7 of 9". When the rung is full it says "Today already has nine my-now cards. Finish
  one, or move one to this week."

## 4. What reads it, and who sees what

Nothing about visibility changes. This is the 2026-08-22 settlement, written into `function:ahead` 4 and 5:

- A hand is its holder's alone to write. Nobody can put a card in it, move it, or finish it.
- Everything is readable. /walk/<project> reads `ahead:<project>:*`, every person's hand in that project,
  rather than every hand on the beach filtered by field 2.
- A finished card lands where it always has, in the holder's own reading of the project (`<project>:<handle>`)
  at the moment it closed. The project is now read off the hand's name rather than field 2. Others in the
  project are offered it to confirm, and the confirmation is a node in their own reading.
- A card is pointed at as `ahead:<project>:<handle> <address>`. /tracks reads the holder off the name's last
  segment.

## 5. Moving what stands

- **Each hand moves itself.** The first time its holder opens /hands with their key on the device, the open
  cards of `ahead:<handle>` land in the project hands at the same rung. Each card lands first, keeping its own
  stamp, and only then is its old slot emptied, the order /next's ↑ keeps. Done and dropped cards stay where they are as the
  record. The old hand is set aside, never wiped.
- **Nothing writes another person's hand.** On 12 October seven hands stand: Phenomemental 16 open cards,
  happyseaurchin 15, Dwayne 6, Duke 4, happyhedgehog 2, Mark Beall 1, weft 1. Each moves on its holder's
  next visit. Until then every page still reads the old hand's open cards beside the new hands.
- `ahead:weft` moves by weft's own key.
