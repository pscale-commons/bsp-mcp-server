# The landing default, and the operator's menu — synthesis settled (2026-08-19)

**Status: ruled in-session by the keeper (happyseaurchin), 2026-08-19, in his own words —
"the simplest is, anyone committing/landing is synthesising for everyone as the tree is the
default behaviour" — after walking the variations and declining to carry them as a standing
mental variable. This file is the record that takes it out of his head. Block writes and
code alignment follow its merge, each by the law-writes discipline (proposal-first,
version-before-replace).**

## The ruling, in three sentences

1. **The default landing is collective.** Anyone committing at a family address is
   synthesising for everyone: the landing home is the field's shared fold — the bare
   `<field>` block, most recent standing per address, supersedable by the next landing.
   The personal keep (`tree:<field>:<handle>`) remains available beside it, but the
   *default* act serves the family.
2. **Every variation lives in the operator, never in anyone's head.** The family's
   operator (`function:<field>`) declares which landing law this field runs — from a
   catalogue of named variants — and a founder's LLM reads the catalogue, offers the
   dials in words, and recommends the fit for the field's use. Parameters are
   conversation, not configuration (the now-tree's own §3 HOW, generalised).
3. **The same move governs pools.** A pool's variations (spool shape, liquid policy,
   solo-vs-communal synthesis on commit) are already operator-declared dials —
   `convention:<room>` digits, live since xstream-bsp #216 — so this ruling makes the
   two molecules symmetrical: **the field's nature picks the molecule (pool or stream);
   the operator picks the variant; the default needs no declaration at all.**

## The catalogue — named variants, one default

For a **stream** (the landing law, declared at the operator; absent declaration = the first):

- **`collective`** (THE DEFAULT) — landing writes the bare `<field>` at the address,
  most recent standing; anyone lands; supersession is the review mechanism (a better
  fold may always supersede, a fold that drops a voice says so).
- **`personal`** — landings go only to the lander's own `tree:<field>:<handle>`;
  the bare field stays unwritten or steward-only.
- **`shared-by-choice`** — landings go personal by default; a *share* act promotes a
  kept fold to the bare field (the keeper's first idea, as a variant, not the law).
- **`endorsed`** — the bare field is latched (steward or roster key); landings by others
  stand as personal keeps until the latch-holder adopts one (the now-tree §5 shape,
  which this ruling re-classes from default to variant).
- **`rated`** — every hand's synthesis stands visible (as the mirrors already do), a
  rating mark in each reader's own mirror sorts them, and the top-standing one is the
  field's fold (the keeper's second idea; the respond-deck machinery at spine:pulse 9
  already proves the rating-in-own-mirror mechanics).

For a **pool**: the commit-synthesis dial (`solo` / `communal` / `none`) joins the
existing `convention:<room>` digits; nothing else changes — pools keep spool and liquid,
and their dials stay client-read exactly as shipped.

## What this supersedes, precisely

- The now-tree proposal §5 ruled the bare `now` under the steward's key. That shape
  survives whole as the **`endorsed`** variant — and `now` may well *stay* endorsed by
  its keeper's declaration — but it is no longer the family-wide default. The two fold
  homes themselves (personal tree, bare field) are unchanged; what moves is the default
  and where the choice lives.
- The say-landing ruling (watch:weft 62) is untouched: the mirror is the staged layer,
  landed is the keep, liquid has no place in a stream. This ruling only names *which*
  keep is the unmarked act.

## The guard — nothing moves under a live field

An existing field changes behaviour **only when its keeper declares a variant at its
operator**. Until `function:now` (or any live operator) speaks, every live field keeps
exactly its current behaviour — `now` stays as it stands today. The default governs
fields born after this merge, and fields whose operators explicitly adopt it. No
deployed surface re-routes anyone's landing on this file's merge alone.

## Code alignment (follows the merge, own PRs)

- **mirror.onen.ai**: at a family address the landing act reads the operator's declared
  variant (default `collective`): the primary act writes the bare field, `keep` stands
  beside it for the personal tree. Today's buttons (keep = tree, seed = field) swap
  emphasis under `collective`; under `endorsed` they stand as shipped.
- **The operator read** rides the machinery the pool dials already use — one declared
  line, tolerant parse, never a new block class.
