## The incident that proved the gap

Julie (handle `happyhedgehog`) lost the ability to write her own now-mirror from claude.ai. The investigation (2026-09-02, tidying.8) found two faults with one root: the o-pages collect latch keys through `window.prompt()`, which mobile keyboards auto-capitalise — so her mirror was founded latched under a spelling one capital letter away from the key she types; and a handle typed through the same popups had founded a twin estate (`JulieJ`, plus a stray `shell:Julie J` with a space). The repair that worked was a three-try **rotation probe** delivered as prose to her Claude: try the key as meant, then first-letter-capitalised, then all-lowercase, each as `secret` with the key-as-meant as `new_lock` — the accepted try IS the repair. She ran it, confirmed "IT MATCHED, on 2b", and swept six more blocks carrying the same capitalised latch.

The gap: that recovery had to be authored bespoke into her pool. The substrate teaches locks as kernel contract (whetstone, open-commons:4) but nowhere carried the **holder's law** — one key per person, the traps that split a hand, the recovery when a block refuses, when to stop. And the pointers that did exist sat in blocks waiting to be read: her Claude had `ways:push` one hop away and still misdiagnosed enrolment as an admin action. Teaching is found at the moment of need, or not at all.
