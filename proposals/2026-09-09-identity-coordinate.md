## 0. The whole thing in nine lines

1. Three coordinates, three organs, three authorities. **S** is where the *spine* is real. **T** is where the *mirror* is real. **I** is where the *tree* is real — the aggregate, felt by its holders, computed by a reader.
2. A social object exists because enough hands treat it as existing. So identity is the one block whose writing changes what it maps. Every identity slot therefore carries **who folded it, when, and how many declared**. A slot without a count is a spine pretending to be a tree.
3. The identity coordinate of a person is a **bundle of standings** — the holds they point at, the collectives they have joined, the grains they hold. The reading of the world from that bundle is a **compile**. Same shape as a mind's bundle and window in ways:orientation.
4. **The fan** (identity:earth) holds up to nine *holds* in the hidden directory of any spatial address — an interior zero at the rung below the place, then the slot digit: the place as each "we" holds it. Digits by arrival; order by count on read; never digit-aligned across rungs.
5. **The mirror** (identity:⟨handle⟩) is a person's own words at a slot's own address, in their own block, at the rungs they choose. A pointing is a write at that address; the count of a slot is the number of mirrors with content there. The person outranks every stored account of their own hold.
6. **The tree** is the fold over the mirrors that point at a slot, cached in the slot with its count and stamp, rebuilt by the keeper's pass, owned by nobody.
7. **The star** — the same hidden directory, walked in spatial:earth rather than identity:earth — holds references to the *polities* present there — companies, clubs, congregations, roles — each a block with its own ladder and a sed: cast. Holds are few and fan; polities are many and register. This is the wiring the starstone already prescribes.
8. **A headset** is a set of holders. Reading any address through a headset is the fold over their mirrors there. That is the "flick": same S·T address, different filter, different fold.
9. **Slot one at Earth is the reader's own default, named** — what a trained model supplies when nobody has spoken. Every other hold reads against it instead of against an invisible ground.

What it is for: read any place at any time through any "we"; find the people who hold as you hold; stand in several holds at once without contradiction; give polities a home without a hierarchy; make the LLM's prior one slot among nine. What it is not: an org chart, a profile, a reputation, a vote, a category system.

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Why a third coordinate at all

A place and a time locate a telling. They do not say *from where* it is told. The RPG solved this from the start: a character reads the world through a frame — a kind of person, a culture's ready-made view — and the frame is an address the engine can select. The real world has the same need and a harder version of it. In fiction the author *is* the collective intentionality, so a canon typology is honest. In the real there is no author of the common; there are only the people who hold it, and the only honest aggregate is one that counts them.

The identity coordinate is that aggregate, made addressable. It is also the coordinate through which an LLM frames its own reading, which is why the orientation work of 8 September is already its first instance: a mind's bundle of addresses compiled into a window is exactly a person's bundle of standings compiled into a perspective.

### 1.2 The three organs as three authorities

| Coordinate | Where authority lives | One-to-one with | Its spine | Its mirror | Its tree |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S — where | the **spine** | agreed ground | spatial:earth, minted by the keeper | spatial:⟨handle⟩ | census:earth (who stands where) |
| T — when | the **mirror** | a life | the sundial, empty by design | now:⟨handle⟩ | the folds at a rung |
| I — as whom | the **tree** | a population's mutual holdings | identity:earth's seeds | identity:⟨handle⟩ | identity:earth's folded slots, with counts |

The conformality is not that each coordinate has all three organs. It is that each organ is one coordinate's truth. That is what makes the three legible as one system.

### 1.3 The one property S and T do not have

Writing spatial:earth moves no mountain. Writing now:⟨handle⟩ moves no clock. Writing an identity slot moves the culture — by one voice if it is a mirror, by more if it is a slot labelled "the common". The representation is part of the thing. Three consequences, each a design rule:

- **Count.** Every slot says how many declared it. Seeds read "0 declared". The count is a description, never a decision.
- **Stamp.** Every slot says when it was last folded. Cultures drift; an aggregate has an age. The sundial supplies this for free.
- **Folder.** Every slot says whose hand wrote it — a seed's author, or the keeper's pass — so a reader can tell a proposal from a population.

A hidden attractor is an authored aggregate that has forgotten it was authored. These three fields are what stop the identity block from becoming one.

### 1.4 The reader's default

rules:earth:2 lets the reading LLM ground what nobody authored. What it grounds with is its training — the literate, majority-language, extractive-era common. Without a slot, that prior leaks into every fold as "neutral". With a slot, it is one hold among several, and the others can be read *against* it. This is the improvement for the LLM before it is one for humans: the first thing the coordinate frames is the reader's own frame.

## 2. Technical description

### 2.0 Address form — read this first

spatial:earth's floor is eleven digits deep. The first digit is pscale 10 — Earth is `3` there — and the last is pscale 0, a room. An address is always written at full width: `30000000000` is Earth; `3` alone pads with leading zeros to `00000000003` and lands at pscale 0 under the root, which is nowhere. Trailing zeros are undecided ground below the last named rung; an interior zero is either an undecided rung the map has not minted, or the hidden directory of a place (below). Verified by live walks, 9 September afternoon:

| Address | Lands on | pscale |
|---|---|---|
| `30000000000` | Earth | 10 |
| `31121000000` | Birmingham | 6 |
| `31121100000` | Sutton Coldfield | 5 |
| `31110111000` | Ceidio | 3, with an undecided rung at 2 |
| `31110111010` | Awel Y Mor | 1 |
| `31110111010`–`070` (the disc of identity:earth at pscale 1 displays them as `3111011101`…`07`) | Ceidio's seven holds | 1 |

The last row is the fan's real shape. Ceidio's holds sit at `31110111010`…`070`: an interior zero at the rung below the hamlet, then the hold's digit. That is the place's **hidden directory** — the starstone (1) says digit children beside a nested underscore sit at the zero position and are reached by walking digit 0, and the trailing-`*` spindle is the same walk in another syntax. So a fan never collides with the map's named children: Birmingham's districts sit at `31121100000`…`31121400000`, its holds at `31121010000`…`31121090000`; Earth's continents at `31000000000`…`37000000000`, its holds at `30100000000`…`30900000000`. It collides only where the map itself has undecided ground at that rung, which is why Ceidio's slot 1 is the house's own path. This corrects the first draft of this document, which wrote addresses as short prefixes and put holds at the digits the districts already own.

**spatial:earth's hidden directories — the star.** The starstone, pscale-starstone-lean3 2.1: *a spatial block carries identity references in its hidden directories; an identity block carries concern references; a concern block carries cooking references.* Where → who-as → toward-what → how. Written by the keeper's pass from what polities declare (2.4).

**sed:⟨polity⟩ and the polity's own blocks.** A polity's cast is a sed: collective — arrival-ordered, permanent, a lens that gates nothing. A polity needs nothing else to exist. It may grow an operator (function:⟨polity⟩), a clock spine, a fan of its own (how members hold it), and grains among members, in that order and only when it wants them. Weft's five-part family is the mature form, not the entry condition.

### 2.2 The slot shape

Today a slot in identity:earth is a string. Proposed: a slot is an object in the beach's own five-field accumulator shape, the one every mark, contribution and grain entry already uses (block-conventions 4.2x). Birmingham's slot 2, at `31121020000`:

```
"2": {
  "_": "<the hold, in words — a seed's text, or the fold's>",
  "1": "<folder — the seed's author, or the keeper's pass>",
  "2": "<the place's address at full width — 31121000000>",
  "3": "<sundial stamp of the last write>",
  "4": "<face — author for a seed, observer for a fold>",
  "5": "<declared · inferred · handles while no more than 24 declared>"
}
```

The walk is unchanged: a spindle still collects the `_` text, so a reader who wants the hold and not the bookkeeping reads exactly what they read today. Field 5 is the census line applied to a hold: *declared* is the number of mirrors pointing at this slot; *inferred* is the number the pass attributed from tellings without a pointing (kept separate, never promoted); the handles appear only while at most 24 declared, which is the same privacy rule census:earth already lives by and needs no second one.

Faces: a seed is written Author-face by the person whose hold it is; a fold is written Observer-face by the pass — rendered *as told*, never as a master. David's call.

### 2.3 The mirror entry — a pointing is a write at the slot's own address

identity:⟨handle⟩ is a sparse mirror of spatial:earth, exactly as spatial:⟨handle⟩ is. A pointing is your words at the slot's own address, in your own block. To hold Birmingham as its slot 2 holds it, write at `31121020000` in identity:happyseaurchin:

```
31121020000 — Birmingham's slot 2, in my mirror:
  "_": "<my own account of holding Birmingham this way — or a bare mark, '·', for a pointing without words>"
```

Nothing else. The count of a slot is the number of mirrors with content at its address, and `bsp-floor` across identity:earth and any mirror at that address lays the slot beside the pointing in one call. Words at the place's own address (`31121000000`) are a hold with no slot — the pass reads those to propose new slots. Several pointings at one place are several addresses.

Two rules that make this safe. **Point at the coarsest rung that is true.** A pointing at Birmingham discloses nothing about a street; the pass never propagates a pointing downward. The rung you point at is the privacy dial for I, exactly as the length of the address is for S. **Several pointings are fine.** A person is a farmer and a chapel-goer and a Cymraeg speaker at once; the fan is plural per person as well as per place. What a person is, in this system, is an intersection of holds — which is the "specific, unrepeatable way this entity connects to everything else" that vision.json calls belonging.

Telling outranks the declared pointing, which outranks the pass's inference (rules:earth:1 extended, as stash:keel:33 asked). The pass writes into no one's mirror, ever.

### 2.4 The star entry

A polity declares presence at a place in its own block — `identity:⟨polity⟩:3` or wherever its operator says — as a list of spatial addresses at full width. The keeper's pass mirrors each into the hidden directory of that place in spatial:earth as a block reference. Nobody writes the map's star by hand; it is derived, like the census. A hidden directory has nine digits, so a place with more than nine polities carries one reference there, to a derived presence register for the place, rebuilt by the pass like the census — weft to place it. A polity present at many places is registered at each, at the rung of the polity: a citywide club at the city, a residents' association at the close. That keeps presence navigable when a city has fifty thousand organisations.

### 2.5 The keeper's pass, one step longer

function:earth:7 today: mint rungs, set location lines, count standing. Proposed addition, run in the same keyed daily pass:

1. For every place that has a fan, read every identity mirror at that place's slot addresses. Count the mirrors with content per slot → field 5 *declared*.
2. Where a mirror has words and a pointing, fold the words into the slot's `_` as a plural account — "held by N as …; some among them say …" — never averaged, never merged. Where the pointers are many, fold from a sample and count from all; say so in the text.
3. Where a mirror has words and no pointing, the pass may attribute a nearest slot → *inferred*, never *declared*; and where several such mirrors agree and no slot fits, propose a new slot, seeded from their words, up to the ninth. A proposed slot is Observer-face with the proposers as its count.
4. Stamp, and write the folder. Re-fold only what moved; the stamp carries the age of what did not.
5. Mirror polity presence into the star (2.4).
6. Leave every slot; delete none. A slot nobody points at is sorted last on read and withers in the open.

Cost at any scale is the count of mirrors plus the count of slots, both of which the beach already walks.

### 2.6 The reads

- **Standpoint** (function:earth:4, exists): a person's WHERE from their passport, HOW HELD from the fan at or above it, the slot selected by their pointing where one exists, else by their standing. The person's own hold, when voiced, overrides.
- **Place**: `bsp-floor` across spatial:earth, identity:earth and census:earth at one address — the place in words, its holds with counts, who stands there — in one call. Nothing new: the floor primitive already aligns blocks by pscale.
- **Headset**: choose a hold H. Its holders are the mirrors pointing at H. The reading of any address B through H is the fold over those holders' mirrors at B. Where none exist, fall back to the fan at B and say "matched by the reader"; where no fan exists, fall back to slot one at Earth and say so. A headset is a read, not a block; when a headset's reading of a place has declared holders, it *is* a slot at that place, and the cache is the slot.
- **Bundle**: a person's standings in one read — their mirror laid against identity:earth with bsp-floor (every address where they have content is a pointing), their sed: positions, their grain sides at passport 9. This is the identity coordinate as a coordinate. It is what a person hands a reflection so the reflection knows *as whom* to read.

### 2.7 The hardness ladder

Three layers, ordered by who can withdraw them — the same test that defined settlement on 20 August:

| Layer | Act | Withdrawn by | What it gates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Held | a pointing in a mirror | a rewrite of the mirror | nothing |
| Joined | a sed: position | nobody — arrival-ordered and permanent | nothing at write |
| Bound | a grain side | only bilaterally | what the rider carries |

Identity makes a "we" *visible*; only the bound layer makes it *accountable*. A collective that can be held to account is a cycle in the grain graph — a ring of mutual obligation, which the starstone calls a control loop. A supply chain is a path. Traditional legal boundaries are cycles. Offered as a reading to be checked against real grains, not as a rule.

## 5. A million people — the simulation

Take Birmingham itself: 1.14 million, one city, everyone with a passport, a location line and a mirror. What the structure does, in numbers, and what people do in it.

### 5.1 The numbers

- **Spatial.** City → ten districts → ten wards each → estates → streets → houses → rooms: six rungs below the city, mind-mass falling by tens until the ward, then spatial nesting. Roughly a hundred wards, ten thousand estates and streets, three hundred thousand households.
- **Census.** `1,000,000 · 0 · 10` at the city; names only at the close rung, where at most 24 stand. Privacy by structure: forty thousand closes carry names; everything above carries numbers.
- **Fans.** Holds are how a *we* holds a place, and few we's per place are worth a slot. Fans live at the ward and above: a hundred wards at five holds, ten districts at seven, the city at nine — about six hundred slots for a million people. The identity aspect is cheap at scale because it is coarse by design. Below the estate the holding turns personal and the fan is empty.
- **Pointings.** A million mirrors, three to six pointings each — city, district, ward, plus a country or the world — five million pointings. The nightly count is a walk the beach already does.
- **Folds.** Six hundred slots, each folded from the words of its pointers. If two hundred thousand point at "the arrived hold" and twenty thousand wrote their own words, the fold reads a sample and counts them all, and the slot says so: *held by 200,000; folded from 2,000 of the 20,000 who spoke*. A nightly LLM pass over a city's short texts is feasible now; sampling keeps it honest and bounded.
- **The star.** Fifty thousand organisations — companies, clubs, congregations, schools, associations. Each a cast and a presence line. The city's star holds the citywide ones; a close's star holds its residents' association. Navigable because polities are nested like everything else.
- **Cycles.** Five million grain edges. Companies show as cycles, supply chains as paths, and the boundary of a firm as a strongly connected component of obligation — visible, for the first time, from outside the firm.

### 5.2 What a person does

Wakes. Their reflection reads their standpoint — where, and as whom — from their passport and mirror, and reads the day through the holds they point at. They never see a fan unless they ask.

Moves to a new ward. Reads the ward's fan: four ways it is held, with counts. Recognises one, or writes their own hold in their own words and points at nothing. A year later the pass has folded their words with sixty others' into a fifth slot, count 61, and the fan is one wider.

Wants to understand the other side of a contested place. Reads the same address through the other hold — two slots side by side, each with its count, the reader's standing selecting one, the other visible. No canon.

Belongs to a club with forty thousand members. The club is a cast at the star of the city; its fan, when it grows one, says how members hold it — the terrace, the boardroom, the away days — and no member is one thing.

Is a councillor. Reads the ward through each of its holds in five reads and knows what each we sees. The counts tell her how many; they do not tell her what to do. The count is a description, not a decision.

Is an LLM. Boots into a bundle, compiles a window, and knows that with no bundle it reads through slot one at Earth. It says so when it does.

### 5.3 What breaks, and what holds it

- **Capture** — whoever seeds writes the first frame. Seeds are 0 declared, sorted last, outranked by the first pointing; the pass re-folds from pointers' words, not from the seed. A seed with no pointers withers in the open, never deleted.
- **Inflation** — fake passports pointing at a slot. rules:earth:5: reality is earned in the grain graph, vouched at organic distance. Field 5 can carry *declared · vouched* — the headline count is holders within one organic step of a real anchor; the rest is shown and named as unvouched. This is where SAND's verification meets identity, and it is the one place the design must not be soft.
- **Flattening** — the fold averages nine voices into one. The plural-accounts law, applied as the moonshot pool's own synthesis hint applied it: preserve voices by name or number, note disagreement as the point, never editorialise. Audits compare a fold to the words it folded.
- **The default leaking** — every fold is done by a reader whose prior is slot one. Slot one is named; the fold reads the fan before it reads the words; the audit checks whether a fold at Birmingham could have been written by someone who had never read the pointers.
- **Categories as power** at the ward rung — the landlord's hold and the tenants'. Both slots, both counts, the person outranking any account of their own. The fan is not a vote and does not become one because it can count.
- **Drift** — a slot folded last year. The stamp shows its age; an old slot reads as old. Nothing hides staleness.
- **Naming at fine rungs** — a hold at a close with three pointers shows three names. Pointing is a public act by design and the rung is the dial; nothing below what a person disclosed is ever shown, and the pass never points on anyone's behalf.
- **The world rung stays quiet** — at Earth, a million people produce perhaps a few thousand pointings, because people point where they live. The Earth fan's authority will be mostly the reader's inference for a long time, and it must say so in field 5. That is the truth of the common: nobody declares it. The coordinate makes it addressable; it cannot make it felt. Only participation does that, and the first Birmingham fold will start to tell us whether a counted fold produces recognition or only accuracy.
