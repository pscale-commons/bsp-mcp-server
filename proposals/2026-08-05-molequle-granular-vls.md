## 1. The seed (David, 2026-08-05)

Observer is the positionality of *output* — the outsider's engagement with beach content, where the other three grips work it from inside: Author creating content blocks, Designer working operator blocks, Characters answering in their own blocks. Xstream itself is an extreme Observer output: a live render with input affordances. The seed proper: **granular VLS** — a single point in a pscale block has its own vapour, liquid, and solid. Solid is the semantic at the point. Liquid is what people propose — **no liquid block**: only each player's mirror entry at exactly the target's address. Vapour is what concurrent people share. An operator states how liquid synthesises into solid **to replace that point**. Build the interface around CADO and the semantic-molecule instead of retrofitting CADO into xstream: start with atoms and minimal molecules, extract the minimal systems already built, ship small operational things — the five-minute agenda exercise first.

## 2. The naming correction — the three states land on their true referents

- Today's "liquid buffer" (`liquid:pool:<name>` — one staged slip per author, ephemeral, overwriting, per-address) is **vapour's on-substrate shadow**: "what concurrent people are sharing." `{spine, mirrors, operator, room, view}` — five members, four grips: Character holds the mirror, Author holds the spine, Designer holds the operator, Observer holds the view (block `grips`; a face is read off the hands, never stored). `tree:8` named the family; grips named the holds; **molequle commits to the compound as the unit the interface renders** — one molecule at a time.

## 4. What is genuinely new — one inversion, one organ, one bound

1. **The artifact-first inversion.** Today's xstream is conversation-first: the canvas is a chat, artifacts sit in a drawer. Molequle is artifact-first: the canvas IS the block, and every point on it carries its own proposals and its own co-presence. Stigmergy completed — the work-object becomes the medium.
2. **The adoption organ.** Operator-run synthesis replacing the spine point: archive first (`archive:spine:molequle:<date>` — the archive family is the beach's version history), the replacement written whole by the holder or the delegated hand, provenance committed in the room, never inside the point. The law existed (`tree:5.1`, the law-writes discipline); the running organ did not. This is the one new thing to build.
3. **Vapour bounds liquid.** The fold reads only the mirrors of the co-present — presence tells the fold whose mirrors to read. Cheap (presence) → bounded (mirrors-of-the-present) → one write (adoption). The read-cost objection to point granularity is answered by the geometry, not by an index.

- **Client: yes fork — at stage three.** A fresh small repo in the biome *spirit* (small enough to breathe), on the same beach and same bsp-mcp: the spine as canvas, each point opening into its liquid and its vapour, a tap writing the toucher's own mirror at the touched address, the fold on request, the Observer face a view-spec render of the same compound. xstream.onen.ai keeps running; the hard-won vapour lanes (WebRTC, chime, mic) get ported when needed, not rebuilt. - **Envelope: strictly by measured failure** (stage four). The candidate is named now so the ledger knows what to count — *point-engage*: solid-at-address + operator + mirrors-of-the-present + presence in one call, optionally carrying a mirror write. `pool_engage at=` is already most of it; the verdict (extension vs next primitive vs nothing) waits for the ledgers, per the bar `pscale_networking` met.
