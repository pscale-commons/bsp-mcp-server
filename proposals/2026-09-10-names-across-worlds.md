## 1. What already agreed

Every world with its own surface already holds its registers as the aspect word plus the world's name at
that surface: `spatial:earth · identity:earth` at earth, `spatial:urb · temporal:urb · identity:urb ·
keeper:urb` at urb, `spatial:brackenfoot · identity:brackenfoot · keeper:brackenfoot · rules:brackenfoot ·
roster:brackenfoot · frame-spec:brackenfoot` at `/w/brackenfoot`. The worlds register at the apex routes a
bare name to its surface. `identity:urb` and `identity:earth` say the same thing in their own words: who
holds a place and how, at scale. David's map mirror at earth is `spatial:happyseaurchin`, the right form.

The gap was at the person's end. Three now-lines at the apex have no passport behind their handle
(`now:David` where the passport is happyseaurchin; `now:Phenomimental`, a misspelt twin of Phenomemental;

the handle when the line was written; and nothing said what a person's mirror of the identity register is
called, or that the self is never a mirror.

## 2. The law (world-genome 2.5; block-conventions 4.7.6)

1. **A world is a surface.** Its registers are `<aspect>:<world>` at the world's own surface — spatial,
   temporal, identity; keeper the held one.
2. **A person's mirror of a register is `<aspect>:<handle>` at the same surface**, node for node at the
   register's addresses: `spatial:<handle>` for their places in their own words, `identity:<handle>` for how
   they hold each place. The handle is a passport at that surface, or a roster entry at a table. The
   world's word never enters a mirror's name; the surface says the world.
3. **The self is never a mirror.** `passport:<handle>` and `shell:<handle>` are the self (shell-genome).
   *Identity* means who holds a place and how, at scale — never a self-description.
4. **Names are derived from the passport, never typed.** A door that founds a mirror reads the passport at
   that surface first. Ruled: the page check now (`/now` in happyseaurchin-home refuses to found a line for a
   handle with no passport there and offers the nearest spellings; a mirror already standing is shown as it
   is); the beach-handler gate only if strays keep appearing — it would make the passport a handle's first
   block, which the `/now` flow does not guarantee today.
5. **The clock's mirror stays `now:<handle>`.** The sundial is the router's, not a world's register;
   renaming to `temporal:<handle>` would touch the page, both wakes and the router for no gain. Ruled: keep.
6. **The strays stay.** Ruled: leave them.

## 3. The amendment — the perspective spine is authored first

The Author face of the family, and not a special phase: a spine has a holder who writes it, a mirror is
anyone's voice at the same address, the tree is the fold, and the holder adopts from the fold by their own
write (tree:5.1). Every spine starts authored; generation begins the day the fold outweighs the author's
word, and nothing is rebuilt then — the spine keeps what was adopted, the tree keeps what the sweep folded,
and a page shows the fold as a fold wherever the spine is silent.

- **`identity:earth` is the perspective spine, David's.** Its lock rotated from the keeper's operational
  lock to his own handle key on 2026-09-10 (the text at the hand-over stands at
  `archive:identity:earth:2026-09-10`). The root's own sentence saying so is his to add with his key — the
  first edit `/here` makes — and reads, or better: *this block is the perspective spine of the real and its
  holder authors it; the keeper's pass reads it and never writes it, and the fold of everyone's perspectives
  with its stats is computed beside it, never into it; a person's own perspectives at these addresses are
  their identity mirror, identity:<handle> at this beach, spelt as their passport spells the handle; the self
  is never a mirror.* One fan per place one pscale below it, at most nine, five to seven authored, read as the
  ring and never walked through. Where a perspective would have folded from people's mirrors, the mirrors are
  named by handle, never copied.
- **The sweep writes the tree beside it, never the spine.** The fold with its stats — how many voices, which
  mirrors, when — is derived and unlocked like the census; the census is that tree, extended, when the
  line-builder beside `scripts/earth-census.mjs` exists. An authored count is a claim; a folded one is a stat.
- **The person's identity mirror returns**: `identity:<handle>` at earth, the same kind as `spatial:<handle>`
  in the map family; optional until wanted.
- **`function:earth` amended** (the text before at `archive:function:earth:2026-09-10`): 7 no longer keys
  the holds to the keeper; 7.3 is the read-only pass over the ring and the mirrors; 7.4 names the exact blocks
  a person writes and how (whole nodes, never a bare string over a zero chain); 7.7 moves the anchors and
  earns the spine's author the same reply as any holder; 4 names the spine and the mirror; 9 records it.
  `char-creation` 2.3 says the names at the door. The evening wake's step 8 reads the spine and never writes it.
- **Urb is the same procedure.** Authored `spatial:urb` and `identity:urb` now; the characters' synthesis
  trees beside them when they are worthy; the same adoption law. `identity:urb`'s root sentence is David's
  hand; the line, to paste or better:

  > A person's own perspectives at these addresses are their identity mirror — identity:<handle> at this
  > surface, spelt exactly as their passport or the roster names them; the self is never a mirror, it lives in
  > the shell and the passport. This register is authored first, as every register is, and the fold of the
  > players' own perspectives is computed beside it when their trees are worthy, never written into it.
