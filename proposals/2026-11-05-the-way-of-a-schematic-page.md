## 3. Why it is shaped this way

- **One way per form, not one way with cases.** A maker reading `ways:proposal` for a schematic has to hold two shapes in mind and guess the mapping at every step; a parallel block with the same addresses lets a maker, or a builder briefed from it, read one block and find the proposal's step at the same address where more is wanted.
- **The faces and the dial are the schematic's whole point**, and a crafted page that lost them would be a proposal page with pictures. The way keeps them as the form keeps them: two choices the reader makes, nothing stored.
- **The check is the plain page's reader.** proposal-test has no schematic cases, and writing a battery for one form's pages before two pages exist would be a blueprint; the way says what the check is today and where the battery will go.
