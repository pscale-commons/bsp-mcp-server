```
25,043 chars  →  7,181 chars   (71% smaller)
```

Every client paid the 25k at connect, whatever it had come to do — a session hosting a person
loaded the lock rules and the address invariant; a builder loaded the Character-face guidance.
That is precisely what the law names as the fault.

## What stays

- **The primitive** — signature, the shape-derivation table, the address invariant in two
  sentences, locks in one. This cannot live anywhere else: it is how the one function is called.
- **The dispatch** — the three targets and the translation, plus discovery in a clause.
- **One paragraph for the clock** — the now-stamp, behind-is-record / AHEAD-is-intention,
  indexicals bound at write time, full-width sundial spindles. The ladder and the comparison law
  are a pointer to `pscale://sundial`.
- **One paragraph for the face** — the four CADO faces and the rule that a Character or Observer
  human never sees `bsp()`, spindles, "settle" or "sed:".
- **Orientation by face**, which the law asks for and the text did not have: a person present →
  `pscale_invite` and the welcome, *never* the whetstone read at them; a builder → the whetstone;
  a waking mind → `pscale_genus` and its recipe; a handle entering a world → `pscale_play` and its
  directive. One route each.

## What moved to blocks

The five long passages that a block already teaches, now pointers: where a person's words land
(`whetstone:8.5`), locating someone's words before writing them (`soft-agent:6.7`),
order-and-collect (`grit:7.4`), the five lock rules (the `bsp` tool schema states them, and
`whetstone` 4 teaches them), and the per-primitive prose — **each primitive's own schema already
carries its description**, so the router repeating it was paying twice for one sentence.

The sentinel list keeps its names and one clause each, because that list is the pointer.

## A staleness bug this surfaced

The text announced **"six substrate primitives"** and listed six, omitting
`pscale_stream_engage` — which has been registered since 2026-08-10. Twelve tools are registered;
the connect text described eleven. A connecting client was told a tool it can call does not
exist. Fixed, and a check now asserts every registered name appears.

## One deliberate deviation, named rather than buried

The law's list does not include the **WHAT THIS IS** posture paragraph, and I kept it (compressed
from ~900 to ~600 chars). Reason: it is the sentence that stops "secret", "register", "settle" and
"agent" being pattern-matched to risk by a fresh model, and losing it costs refusals on ordinary
civilian work. It is also the only paragraph whose *absence* cannot be repaired by a pointer,
because a session that has already misread the vocabulary will not go and read `open-commons`.
If you would rather the law be applied strictly, say so and I will cut it to a single sentence
plus the pointer.
