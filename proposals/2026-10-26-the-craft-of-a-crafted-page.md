**Lane**: weft.parlour, Claude Code (Opus 5.5) on the Mac, at David's word

## The ask

David, 26 October 2026, passing on keel's suggestion and its crafted-page skill:

> *"improve the proposal instructions; this is what keel suggests to provide better instructions to LLM; I've also attached a skill it has produced, which I think should be converted into some kind of recipe/cook thing which an LLM can find if it is looking to create a proposal; either within BSP-mcp sentinel and/or a doc linked from the page."*

keel's suggestion was to put the standard where sessions read first. It gave one line for the proposal form, and a skill covering the design brief, the beach wiring, the checks and the handoff to weft.

  Each step's 2 to 7 hold its detail. Two lessons from the same day are folded in. At 1.111: only the repo page goes to the site; a claude.ai preview export, handed over in its place, lacks the site's head and scripts. At 1.111113 and 1.111114: what weft does with a page, and what it names on the beach once the page is live. The block is under the shared project lock, so David can correct it directly. It reads as a page at `happyseaurchin.com/explain?block=ways:proposal`.
- **`function:proposal` 4**, the page. keel's line, verbatim: *"A crafted page wears a look of its own taken from what it proposes: its own palette and type, a drawing that moves at the top, an instrument for each step, and plain words for someone new to the beach. Then a pointer to `ways:proposal`, with its readable address.
- **`function:proposal` 7**, making one. Its last sentence now says a crafted page is made as `ways:proposal` says: its maker leaves it whole at `files:<name>`, weft commits it, and it goes up at David's word.
- **`function:proposal` 9**, the provenance. It gains the amendment and David's words.

## Why a beach block, not a sentinel

The recipe is happyseaurchin.com's, not the protocol's. It names that site's scripts (`theme.js`, `voice.js`, `threads.js`, `/api/mirrors`), its repository, and weft's commit. A sentinel is teaching every beach serves. A sentinel is also code the router reads, so adding one redeploys the router and ends every live MCP session.

A `ways:` block has neither cost. Any LLM reaches it through bsp from the form that already sends it there, and a person reads it at `/explain?block=ways:proposal`.

If a protocol-level crafted-page standard is ever wanted for every beach, the block can be lifted into a sentinel then, as it stands.

## One reading to keep beside it

keel's line says *never the maker's house look*. On 24 October David ruled (pool:weft 286) that the generic page may wear its maker's own look, `look:<handle>`. The two don't collide. The ruling dresses the generic page, drawn from blocks. keel's line is about a crafted page, whose look is read from what it proposes, never carried over from its maker's last page.
