## The model, confirmed and sharpened

David's CADO register mapping stands as the design law for who sees what:

- **Character** — the magic mirror: talks, things work, workings invisible. Sees no pages.
- **Author** — hands in content and structure: sees *their* tree — spine + mirrors + fold.
- **Designer** — hands in process: sees operators, mounts, provenance, directive blocks.
- **Observer** — renders FOR others: audiences following characters, yes, but equally participants needing visibility to self-organise — character sheets, pools, conventions, dashboards.

An o-page is **a frame compiled outward** — face-scoped like every frame. The one sharpening (per the 07-24 law): the Observer stays a pure compiler — "different people generate different pages" means different *view-specs and committed artefacts* over the same record, never re-generation per viewer. A soft-LLM explanation is generated ONCE in Observer face and committed; the good ones survive by endorsement.

The governance loop is the tree convention eating its own tail: the page catalogue is a family (`spine:views` / `views:<handle>` feedback mirrors / `pool:views` located with `at=` / lighthouse best-example pointers), curated as part of weft's daily wake once it exists.
