# History is the narration — a character's account holds the rendering of each beat (2026-09-17)

Lane rpg-o-page.5 (weft; Claude Opus 5 in Claude Code). This is a law-class write (sentinels read by every door), made proposal-first. The standing texts are quoted verbatim below, and git keeps the versions. Coordinated with rpg-mirror.4 through the shell: `watch:weft` 398.51, 398.52, 399.54.

## 0. David's rulings, 2026-09-17 afternoon

In his words, after the character page was found writing comments into `history:Ugarth` by hand:

- **History is the narration.** "History should be the rendering I think (though without an LLM this needs to be the resolution I guess!!!)"
- **No commentary.** "I'm not interested in comments — stash is the right place if the player wants to record something." And before that: "I have never asked for commentary on Ugarth, ever."

The genome already made a character the same shell as any handle: passport, purpose, history and stash, with `witnessed` and `knows` as their legacy names (shell-genome 6.1, 6.13). What it said an entry *is* did not match what every door had come to write:

- **The law said:** a zeroth-person record, one entry at the close of each rendered beat.
- **Every door wrote:** the rendering itself, in second person, located `pool:<room>:<slot>`. That is the mirror (xstream-bsp #310), the doorman (waker `render_for`), and the page that reads both (happyseaurchin-home #272).

The law now says what the doors do, and David has ruled that this is right.

## 1. The amendments

### shell-genome 6.12

Standing:

> history:<handle> is the journal organ, born with the arrival journal as its first entry and carrying the accumulation law at 1 verbatim. Its entries stay per-subject and zeroth-person — what the character witnessed and did, one at the close of each rendered beat, never the seat's envelope echo — and the zero-slot summary falls due at UPKEEP, when grit 3 settles the sheets.

Becomes:

> history:<handle> is the narration, born with the arrival journal as its first entry and carrying the accumulation law at 1 verbatim. Each later entry is the RENDERING of a beat for the character — the moment as they lived it and their player received it — located at the beat it tells (pool:<room>:<slot>) and written by whichever LLM rendered it: the player's own portal while they play, the character's doorman while they are away, a seat's own hand at an LLM-app door (grit 1.24); never a note by hand, never the seat's envelope echo. Where no LLM renders, the resolution stands in the narration as it landed and nothing is written — the pool already holds it. The zero-slot summary falls due at UPKEEP, when grit 3 settles the sheets.

### shell-genome 6.13

Standing:

> stash:<handle> is the curated knowledge a character arrives holding — genesis seeds it with the arrival knowledge, and play earns the rest. witnessed:<handle> and knows:<handle> are the legacy names of the pair; existing doors read both.

Becomes:

> stash:<handle> is the curated knowledge a character arrives holding — genesis seeds it with the arrival knowledge, and play earns the rest — and the one place a player keeps anything of their own about the character, by choice: a character carries no commentary. witnessed:<handle> and knows:<handle> are the legacy names of the pair; existing doors read both.

### grit 1.24 — displaced, not accreted

1.24 lies on the render spine that every door reads at `1.2`. The spine stood at 1,446 words against its ratchet of 1,450 (`smoke:grit-tree`), so the new line keeps the old length, and who journals goes one level deeper.

## 2. What does not change

- **shell-genome 4.** An instance's history is still the automatic record of its own sessions and wakes. 6.12 is the character tier's reading of it.
- **The account's name.** `history:<handle>` is the name, and `witnessed:<handle>` stays a legacy name every door reads. Ugarth's own blocks are renamed separately, at David's go, under Ugarth's key.
- **Without an LLM nothing is copied.** The resolution already stands in the pool, and a narration shows the untold beat raw. Meaning moves by reference.
