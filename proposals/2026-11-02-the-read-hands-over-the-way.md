## What changes

**A read gives the way once in a session.** The first read or say of a family whose law names its way prints the way under the law's section. Later reads in that session say it stands, exactly as the law's section already does: `standing as it was given to you earlier in this session`. It is the same mechanism (`standingBrief`), so it is also given again when the way changes, or after two hours, and a caller with no session gets it every time, as it gets the law.

```
# The way — ways:thread, how a mind carries this family's work; given once in a session
THE WAY OF A THREAD — how a mind carries a piece of work across its own loops, other minds and people. …

1 FIND IT, OR OPEN IT. …
…
8 WELL FORMED, as the battery checks it: …
```

What is printed is the way's root and its steps, without its provenance, which teaches nothing: exactly the text keel tested at 40 of 40 (`thread-test-materials/teachings/way_w2.txt`). `smoke:thread` holds the two equal.

**Both doors give it, and count it as one.** `pscale_stream_engage` prints it in the envelope. `pscale_play(…, thread=…)` prints it in THE THREAD's section. A session that took a thread up at the door is not handed it again at its first read.

**The waker carries it.** A wake cannot fetch, and the waker asks the door in a fresh session, so the way is always in the section it compiles. The section is now about 3,800 characters longer, so the ring's `thread` field grows from 6,000 to 12,000 characters at the waker and the kernel, and the kernel's line says the way is there. Kernel change, upstream-owed to pscale-biome src/agent (parked; noted per the port discipline).

## Who says which family has a way: the survey

Nothing in a handler names `thread`. The block has to say it, and three readings were checked against the live beach:

| Rule | Which live reads it changes |
|---|---|
| a family has a way when `ways:<field>` stands | `thread`, and also `arcade`, `games` and `views`, which each have a `spine:` a stream read can address |
| a law names its way when its root line mentions `ways:<name>` | `thread`, and also `cairn-plug`, `films`, `ghost-race`, `ghost-racer-plug`, `lens`, `studio` and `views`, whose root lines mention a way in passing |
| **a law names its way when its root line closes `is ways:<name>.`** | **`thread` only, of the 141 laws on the beach** |

The third is the smallest, and it is what stands. `function:thread`'s root line already closes *"How a mind carries one, step by step, is ways:thread."*, so the law does not change. Any other family can take this up by closing its own root line the same way, and nothing else changes for it until it does.

## What it costs

Measured with this code on the live blocks, read-only: the way's section is 3,792 characters, where the block raw is 5,138, because its provenance is left at its address. A first read at `thread:2.11211` runs 6,114 characters, 2,322 of them what it printed before. Later reads in that session grow by one line of about 120, and the door's section runs 4,824 characters where it ran about 1,030. `arcade`, `games`, `views`, `now` and `proposal` read exactly as before. Other families' reads don't change at all: the rule needs only the law the read already loads, and fetches the way only for a law that names one. Each step is paid by the mind that takes it, and nothing puts the cost on one hand or one server.

No tool's wording changes, so claude.ai doesn't re-ask.
