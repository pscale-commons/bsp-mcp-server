# The now-tree — the subjective fold, and the magic-mirror as context engine (2026-08-18)

**Status: designed in-session with David (happyseaurchin) 2026-08-18, the day after the now
family stood up (proposals/2026-08-17-the-now-mirror.md). Every ratification his; the open
points at §9 carry weft's preference at each, per his ask. Build waits on the trigger at §8 —
a second human filling her now page. Lane record: watch:weft 79 (the intake) and onward.**

## 1. The distinction that seats everything

A **project tree is object-oriented**: one authored spine, mirrors answering it, the fold
measuring the project against its own declared future. The **now-tree is subjectively
sourced**: `spine:now` is deliberately empty of content — time has no holder — so when
now-mirrors fold there is no objective to measure against, only the shared moment. The clock
is the one thing everyone holds in common; everything else is voice. The subjective fold
therefore *reveals adjacency* where the objective fold *measures progress*. In the keeper's
words: this difference "landed me in the seat of what I am doing and why — the magic-mirror
as context engine for humans, finally." Twenty years of design surfacing (sqale onward);
xstream's original purpose — a socially reflexive mirror — reached at last.

## 2. The standpoint law — there is no global tree

A fold across now-mirrors can only be read *from somewhere*: a person, at a moment, holding a
chosen few beside themselves. A million users never fold; nine neighbours do, a million times
over, overlapping. Global coherence, if it comes, emerges from overlapping standpoints the way
a murmuration does — no centre, no view from nowhere, nothing for authority to displace onto.
Scoping is not a throttle imposed on the read; it is what a subjective read *is*.

## 3. The four axes — WHO · WHY · WHAT · HOW

**WHO — computed frames, not curated lists.** The existing organs *are* the ready-made
circles (the keeper's reversal of the first draft, and it dissolves the curation problem):
a grain names a pair; a sed collective names its registrants; a pool names the co-present;
a project family names everyone whose mirror voices in it; the RPG party names itself. The
WHO is *derived on the spot*, never maintained. A hand-picked list remains available as one
more kind, for whoever wants one — a follow-list, public like everything here, and a person
may hold different ones for different reads. This is the GRIT frame transposed from world
to society: a frame whose references are mirrors — perception scoped socially, beyond the
few who happen to share a room.

**WHY — the placement of the address relative to now.** Three reads, one act (read the same
address across the frame's mirrors), differing only in where the address sits:

- **presence-now** — the now spindle, the primary: where is everyone, right now, ladder
  beside ladder, coarse to fine, silence honest;
- **prospect-ahead** — the cells ahead: who intends what — overlap, and complementarity
  (one hand's need against another's capacity);
- **correspondence-past** — the cells behind: what each actually was — the rhymes.

The geometry the keeper ratified: past and future are relative to now **at each pscale** —
within the same nesting, the ring of siblings around now's cell carries the near past and
near future; **added rings give distance, measured in the units of the attended rung**. A
"week away" and "a season away" are one mechanism at two apertures. And different tempos
meet at coarse rungs by construction: two people who never voice beats still stand together
at the week — pscale aligning lives with no negotiation.

**WHAT — the aperture, which is the price dial.** Point, spindle, subtree, whole mirror.
Cost of one synthesis = roster × aperture × question. Two mature mirrors melded whole —
small roster, deep aperture, open question — is an Opus-grade call and worth it: two lives
laid against one coordinate, possibly the most insightful thing the beach can hand two
people meeting. A hundred-thousand-voice vote — huge roster, point aperture, minimal
payloads — is *also* one call. Same engine, three orders of magnitude apart; economies of
scale derive the scope, built into pscale by design.

**HOW — the operator for synthesis, and the conversational loop.** The synthesis law is
delivered by the family's operator (see §9c) the way every fold law is: the mind that folds
receives the HOW in the envelope, never invents it. When the parameters given are
insufficient, the LLM **defaults, renders, then asks**: sane defaults (frame: the room's
co-present plus standing grains; why: presence-now; what: the spindle) produce *something*
immediately, and the mind then offers the dials in words — "wider? further ahead? just the
two of you?" — until the reflection is the one the person wanted to see. Parameters are
conversation, not configuration.

## 4. The two acts, strictly separated — witnessing, then synthesis

The **snapshot** (the keeper's word: the scoped concatenation, the *witnessing step*) is
computed and free: fetch the frame's mirrors, lay them at the address, attribute every
voice. No LLM anywhere. It is already worth the button — Julie beside you at the same
moment is magic before any mind speaks. The **synthesis** is a mind's act, bound by four
standing laws: it is the *calling* mind's reading, never a central resolver's; it speaks
the participant's register (function:molequle:5 — never an address); it declares its scope
(whose mirrors, which cells — a fold that drops a voice says so); and naming a person in a
fold writes nothing of theirs and nothing *to* them — reaching them is a voice in their
parlour or a rider, deliberate, never blanket (the SAND lesson whole).

## 5. The fold homes — two, and no more

- **Personal kept fold: `tree:now:<handle>`** — the caller's own keeping (#279's law).
- **Public fold: the bare `now` block, at clock addresses, most recent standing** — RULED:
  under the steward's key. The most recent public synthesis at a tree point supersedes in
  place; on the clock spine the address walk IS the sequence (yesterday's fold stands at
  yesterday — no history organ needed, per the say-landing ruling at watch:weft 62).

Everything else is **ephemeral — recomputed, never stored** (views are computed, acts are
committed). This containment is what prevents mirrors-of-mirrors-of-mirrors: exactly two
homes, one personal, one public-most-recent.

## 6. The pyramid — scale by federation, not by supercomputer

A person folds their frame (≤10 mirrors, client-cheap). A beach's bare `now` holds that
community's public fold. A reader across beaches folds *beach-folds*, never raw mirrors.
The keeper's ten passes over a million mirrors happen as federation — distributed to
whoever wants each layer, nobody running the whole. Frame entries are handles,
origin-qualified when a member's mirror lives at another beach — the reference grammar
already exists, so the design is federation-ready from birth.

## 7. The mirror.onen.ai convergence — this read is already half-built

The now-tree read and the mirror-fold read (bsp-mcp#282) are ONE renderer, and its core
shipped 2026-08-17: xstream-bsp #267 (the readings pane — the family's mirrors read back at
the address) and #269 (say is the mirror; landed is the keep), both under the keeper's own
say-landing ruling (watch:weft 62): the mirror IS the staged layer; liquid has no place in
a stream — it is the pool's organ; four blocks, one address space. At any point the surface
shows: the voices (to be frame-scoped — the WHO applied as a client-side filter the
soft-LLM can effect live, since the pane is client-rendered), the standing public synthesis
(bare `now` at the address, once founded), and your own kept fold if any. Vapour is who is
looking now; liquid is only where a pool genuinely stands; solid is the most recent public
synthesis. **Pool-vs-stream is not a user setting**: it is the field's own nature, declared
per field by what the field is (a pool owns a spool and liquid; a family of mirrors is a
stream) — bespoke as a type, exactly as the keeper suspected, and already how the shipped
surface behaves.

## 8. The trigger and the first slice — deliberately small

When a second human (Julie) fills her now page: a **beside** view on /now reading a
computed frame ("your pair · this room · this project's people" — offered in words, no
naming machinery), rendering the presence-now snapshot only. The first syntheses run BY

sounds like before any button automates it (the /opportunities discipline). The bare `now`
block is founded under the steward's key at the first public fold worth keeping. Nothing
else: no new organs, spine:now untouched, the tree computed.

  *preference: yes; distance-in-rings is the ratified geometry, the default stays local.*

  *preference: one operator per family; a second block would be a heading.*
- **(d) `spine:now` to floor 11 (the ten-millennium rung)**: possible any day by supernest

  structural (the zero-collision is between a rung and its living zero-child, invariant
  under floor growth) — *preference: defer until something real needs the rung.*
- **(e) Public-fold reach**: does a fold at bare `now` notify the people it names? —
  *preference: pull-only until asked for; reaching anyone is SAND's job, opt-in per person.*
