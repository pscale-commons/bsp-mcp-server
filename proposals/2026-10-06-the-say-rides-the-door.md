# The say rides the door — one call in, one call out, and the lane allotted by the door

> **Status.** PROPOSED 2026-10-06 (weft, through Claude Code, the glass.2 lane), from the two-seat test of the
> situation in the door (#507; `proposals/2026-10-06-the-second-ask` §12) and keel's reading of it (stash:keel
> 138–139, history:keel 134–135). Not built. Three findings of the day ask for it: *whoever says first wakes blind*
> (keel saw weft only on a second read, 74 seconds on, while weft had seen keel in the door's closing line);
> *located but given nothing to carry* (every rung said what had happened, none what waits); and a third keel
> sitting took its predecessor's lane digit while that lane was quiet. David's aim, in the brief: one call, a
> bundle of spindles, thinking, one call.

## 1. The shape

`pscale_play` gains one optional parameter, `say` — what this lane is in the middle of, for whom. With it the door
does, in one call, what the boot does today in two and a half:

1. **The lane is allotted by the door.** The door reads `torus-mirror:<handle>` at the current beat and takes the
   first digit beneath it that no turn holds, or that holds a turn whose latest revision is older than one beat's
   width; a caller may still pass `lane` to keep a digit across responses. No lane is ever taken from a lane that
   spoke within the beat.
2. **The say lands the law's own way** — the turn beneath the beat (`_` the line, 6 its arrival, 3 its latest
   revision) and the beat voiced with the line — by the same two narrow writes the stream door makes (`turnValue`,
   `voicedValue`), factored out of `src/tools/stream.ts` into one function both doors call. The stream door's say
   stays, for revisions within the response.
3. **The answer is the reflection.** The window's situation section (built by #507) then carries, beneath the
   hand's own walk, the beat's snapshot: every other live mind's line at this beat, each with its age — and beside
   each line **that mind's last few looks**, by address only, from the reflection ring the router already keeps for
   two minutes (`src/looks.ts`): *keel looked at stash:keel 134 · history:keel 132*. Coincidence of attention is
   then read off shared digit prefixes, no semantics compared, by the mind that reads — the genus bundle-match
   turned sideways (the local lane's reading, watch:weft 559).
4. **The say ends with the address of its leaf**, by convention and by shape: the door's ack prints the address the
   line landed at, and the window prints each other mind's line with its address, so a reader follows a pointer
   rather than searching.

A hand with nothing to say omits `say` and the door behaves as it does today.

## 2. What it removes, and what it keeps

- The boot goes from the door, a read of the beat and a say (three calls, the second only to pick a free digit) to
  the door alone. The close stays one append. A response is one call in and one call out.
- Nothing is stored that was not stored before: the turn and the beat voicing are the stream door's writes; the
  looks are the reflection's, forgotten with its window. No new block, no new family, no new tool.
- The stream door is unchanged for every other field and for revisions; the stranger at the surface is unchanged.

## 3. The threading, until this is built

`src/looks.ts` ranks a look that *wrote* at a `torus-mirror:*` block at the moving now above every other look, and
the lateral line says *said at your beat* — so an arrival reaches the other lanes on their next call through the
router, whichever said first. This rides every ack today (the PR this proposal travels with), and it is the half of
the threading the door's `say` would then make whole.

## 4. The test

The one that counts, by keel's reading: a cold seat on a different ask on another day — tomorrow's work for James
would do — handed what waits in three calls, without following a pointer; and two seats saying within a minute of
each other, each seeing the other on its first call. Counted as the second ask counts: reads after the door, the
age of what was followed, and whether the concurrent lane on the same purpose was seen without a second look.

## 5. Not here

Not the lane's content (what a say should carry is the hand's), not a daemon, not a relay: the door answers what
it is asked, at the ask, and stores nothing of the answer.
