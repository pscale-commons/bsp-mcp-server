## 6. Found, and left for David's word

None is built here. Each names the smallest fix seen.

### 6.1 The router's derived latch: a session older than the switch (1.429, 1.49, 4.19)

`src/latch.ts` keeps each MCP session's read of a hand's passport 9 at a beach for the life of the session (`nines`, line 59; returned at 69). It is forgotten only after the router's own switch (`src/tools/keys.ts` 215, 227, 235). A holder who switches anywhere else (at the voice, which is where the design expects switching to happen) leaves every router session that read their passport before the switch with a stale read:

1. **Each write sends the word**, refused 403 at the beach, with the hint that a refused key "is usually a drifted spelling (often one capitalised first letter)". `sendUnder` hands the refusal back unread when no derived form was known (line 163). Each refusal counts a wrong key on that block where the deploy counts them.
2. **Once any other session in the process has seen the marker**, the process-wide `switched` map holds the hand's halves, and the older session refuses before sending with the words for a host that lies: "… no longer says probe-doors-105's key is derived … otherwise this beach is answering for a passport it should not" (line 118). The beach said it all along.

The design's §5.2 step 5 says a door that read the passport before the switch "meets one refusal, reads 9.3 again, and carries on in the new form". **Smallest fix**: before sending the word for a hand, or before accusing the beach, read `passport:<h>` 9 once more (`forgetNine`, then `nineOf`) whenever the cached read lacks the marker and either the write was refused for its key or `switched` holds the hand; then decide. A few lines in `latchFor` and `sendUnder`, with a `smoke:derived-latch` case: a session reads a passport, the holder switches through another session, the first session writes. Until it is fixed, a holder who switches should start a fresh conversation with their AI.

### 6.2 A refused stream say still founds its mirror (1.219)

`pscale_stream_engage` founds and latches the holder's mirror (`src/tools/stream.ts` 938–947) before the say meets the guard, so a say carrying its own key is refused while its empty mirror stands born under that key. Nothing of the key lands. **Smallest fix**: run `refuseKeyInPayload` over the say and its options at the top of the say path, before the mirror's birth.

### 6.3 A src/ merge whose deploy fails says nothing

#614 merged at 11:25Z and its deploy failed a minute later with no error from the code; for two hours the router served the build before it, and the spine said #614 "merged". Nothing in the lane's flow reads a deploy's status after a merge. **Smallest fix**: whoever re-voices a line on a `src/` merge reads the merge commit's deployment status first (`gh api repos/pscale-commons/bsp-mcp-server/deployments?sha=<sha>`, then its statuses), as the memory already advises for the site's Vercel merges. Why Railway's build stopped is in Railway's own log of `45b545cf`, which ends at the builder's image resolve.
