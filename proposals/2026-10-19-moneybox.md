## What changed on landing

Three fixes, without which the family breaks on first use:

1. **The spine is born at floor 10.** Keel's `spine_moneybox.json` was `{"_": "…"}`, floor 1. The stream bears every mirror at its spine's floor (`src/tools/stream.ts`, `bornAt`), so every box would have been born off the clock, and the walker refuses `2026410000.3` on a floor-1 block (left of the decimal exceeds the floor: 400). The spine now stands as `spine:now` does, its line ten underscores deep.
2. **A single-decimal example.** 2.5's `2026410000.3.1` is `2026410000.31`.
3. **A line's first entry is written whole.** An append at a spindle refuses a block not yet born (404) and a node not yet an object, so 7.2's append cannot be a line's first act. A box is now born by its holder's say at the year; a line's first entry in a month writes the line whole; each new counterpart appends.

Found while walking the path, and written into the law:

4. **Never say at a month.** The four lines stand in the month's own underscore: the zeros of `2026410000` walk it down to pscale 0, and the line's digit follows the decimal, clear of the month's digits, which are its weeks. Today a bare line written at a node whose underscore holds a subtree sets that underscore and so replaces the subtree (the beach's `writeAt`, voicing-at-the-terminus; the stream's `voicedValue`). A say at a month would wipe the box's month. The law's root line says so, and the root rides every stream engage. Voicing the deepest underscore of the chain, at the beach and in the stream, would lift this rule; that is not done here.
5. **Nine counterparts a line a month.** The deployed beach refuses a tenth append beneath a digit position ("a digit position holds nine and does not grow"), so a line never wraps and owes no summary inside it. A line holds one entry per counterpart a month, and a further sum to the same counterpart rewrites their entry whole. (The `bsp` tool's own description of `append` still says the node supernests at its tenth.)

Tightenings from the review:

- The pound forward stays beside the GAVE and is never merged with it: SQ reads credit receipts alone (sand-v2 7), and credits never withdraw to money (sand-v2 1.3). Child 4 is a pointer (2.3, 2.5, 8, 8.3).
- Lines stand only at months; the quarter and the year are their sums (7, 7.1).
- A line is placed by its counterpart, not by the position its holder chose, so no pound is counted twice (3.4, 6.2).
- A zero denominator shows a dash (3).
- The comparison with "line-of-sight" is dropped: nothing by that name stands on the beach or the site (1, 1.6).

## How the path was proved

Before the live write, the whole path ran offline against the deployed beach handler and the deployed router (`origin/main` of each, in-process, on a scratch store). It checked the spine at floor 10 and the law whole; a box born at the year, at floor 10, locked to its holder; a line's first entry written whole; an append landing at `2026410000.32` with the beach's stamp at 3; the weeks untouched and the year's voice kept; a further sum rewriting one entry and leaving the other; a line's word said again with its entries kept; the tenth counterpart refused with nothing spilled; the stream at "this month" carrying the law; and a say at the month wiping the lines, which is why the law forbids it. 19 checks, all passing. After the live write, both blocks read back equal to the landed files, and the index lists exactly the two names.

# function:moneybox — the law of the moneybox family

Oct 19, 2026 · @David Pinto

## The underscore — what the family is

The law of the moneybox family: one person's pounds for one month, in a box only they write, read twice, with the collective a fold computed on read and never a pool. A moneybox is always somebody's. There is no collective box, only a reading across boxes.

Each box stands at a month on the canonical clock and holds four lines beneath it: what came in from outside, what came from a member, what went forward to a member, what left to the pound economy. Every number anyone needs is derived from those four at read time — the draw, the coverage, the leak share — for one box, for any set of boxes, or for everyone.

The same four lines carry two voicings, traditional and ecosquared, because the two economies differ in how the pounds are read, not in the pounds. Credits, thanks and SQ are not in this family. The forwarded line names its recipient so that when SAND runs, the record it reads already exists.

## 2 — The box

Beneath a month address a box has four positions, and nothing else.

| Position | Line | What it holds | What it names |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | In | Pounds that crossed into the collective from outside, through this person: an invoice paid, a ticket bought, a backer's transfer, a stipend. | Who from. |
| 2 | Received | Pounds forwarded to this person by a member. The same pound as the giver's line 3. | The giver's handle. |
| 3 | Forwarded | Pounds this person forwarded to a member, with what: a month of beach work, a server kept running, a seat at a table. | The recipient's handle, and the thing. |
| 4 | Costs | Pounds that left to the pound economy: Claude, servers, electricity, food, rent. One sum or several, at the holder's grain. | The kind, if several. |

Each position is an accumulator. An entry appends beneath it (`2026410000.3.1`, then `.3.2`) by the beach's own append, so a month with one inflow and a month with five have the same shape. The position's own line is the law's word for it, or the holder's summary. A thing bought once and then stewarded is not a cost but an enfolding, and is not written here; only its running cost is.

An entry is a sentence at the node and its figures at children: the amount in whole pence at 1, the counterpart (a handle, a source or a kind) at 2, the moment written at 3, and 4 reserved for the SAND probe\_id once a forward and its GAVE are one act. Pence, not pounds, so no fraction ever appears; at Onen's rate a penny is ten credits.

```json
{"_": "£500 to happyhedgehog with October's work on the beach", "1": 50000, "2": "happyhedgehog", "3": "2026-10-19T12:00:00Z"}
```

## 3 — The numbers, derived and never written

Nothing below is stored. For a box in a month, let A, G, F and C be the four lines summed. The draw is what the holder had to attract to do what they did; the coverage says whether it was; the leak share says how much of what reached them left to the pound economy.

```latex
D = C + F \qquad \kappa = \frac{A + G}{C + F} \qquad \lambda = \frac{C}{A + G}
```

κ = 1 sustains the month. κ > 1 leaves pounds undirected. κ < 1 means the month spent what an earlier month brought; the rung above shows which, so no balance is kept.

For any set S of boxes — one person, a project's members, everyone — draw the boundary round S and the same numbers follow. Lines 2 and 3 whose counterpart is inside S pair off, because a forward and its receipt are one pound.

```latex
\mathrm{In}(S)=\sum_{i\in S}A_i+\sum_{i\in S}G_i^{\,\text{from outside }S}\qquad
\mathrm{Out}(S)=\sum_{i\in S}C_i+\sum_{i\in S}F_i^{\,\text{to outside }S}\qquad
\kappa(S)=\frac{\mathrm{In}(S)}{\mathrm{Out}(S)}
```

For everyone there is no outside member, so In = ΣA, Out = ΣC and κ = 1/λ: at the boundary, coverage and leak share are reciprocals. Inside they part, and what parts them is the forwarding. Pounds never multiply inside the boundary; they re-label whose costs are covered. Growth is a new box whose costs are covered, which is boundary expansion.

David's October as he put it in the session — an illustration, not a record: A = £1,000, F = £500, C = £500, so D = £1,000, κ = 1.0, λ = 0.5. The member's box: G = £500, C = £500, κ = 1.0. Everyone: In £1,000, Out £1,000, κ = 1.0 — sustaining, nothing undirected. A third box with £500 of costs needs In to reach £1,500.

## 4 — The two readings

One box, read twice. Traditional subtracts usage from a balance: what is left to spend. Ecosquared subtracts usage from a limit: what one was entrusted to direct, and what is not yet directed. The pounds are identical; the reading changes what a person does next, because paying *for* and paying forward *with* are different acts.

| Line | Traditional voicing | Ecosquared voicing (Onen's words) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 In | Income | Investment credit — credits you've purchased |
| 2 Received | Income from others | Reward credit — credits you've earned |
| 3 Forwarded | Spending on people | Shared credit — net from sharing, negative |
| 4 Costs | Expenses | Credit usage |
| Derived | Balance = income − spending − expenses: money available to spend | Limit = in + received − forwarded; Available = limit − usage |

Onen's credits screen of 19 October 2026 shows the two reconcile: investment 30,000, reward 0, shared −3,703, limit 26,297, usage 16,788, available 9,509 — and in pounds £30.00, £0.00, −£3.70, £16.79, £9.51. The box keeps pence; a reading multiplies by ten for credits, since £1 is 1,000 credits. In the ecosquared voicing, usage is not what Claude's answer cost but what you put toward your ask.

Volume 3 names the difference: "Instead of tracking the moment of exchange, Ecosquared marks a period of intent." The box at the month is that period.

## 5 — The spine

The spine carries what a month asks, never what it got. At each month it may voice the draw the collective needs from outside, as intention: "October asks £1,000 from outside — two people's costs covered." Above the month it is content-free by construction, a template at floor 10 like `spine:now`.

It stands under the shared project lock. David corrects it directly, or Weft writes it at his word; boxes never write it, and a reading of the spine lands in the reader's own box, as `function:document` 3 has it for every family. The October ask already standing at `spine:autumn-revenue` 1 — £10k by 31 October, Robert's sum unwritten — is the kind of line that belongs here, cited by address, never copied.

A month ends when it ends, as `function:backcast` 5 has it. Nothing is cut and nothing carried by hand: what the month did not cover reads as uncovered in the fold, and the year rung carries the rest.

## 6 — The fold

The fold is computed on read and kept by nobody. Given a set of boxes and a rung it does three things, all arithmetic.

1. It sums the four lines across the set and reports κ and λ for the set and for each box, with the draw beside them. The collective row is the set of everyone, where receipts and forwards cancel entirely.
2. It pairs forwards with receipts. A line 3 whose named recipient holds no matching line 2 is a standing gift: presented and aged, never a debt, as `sand-v2` 3.2 holds a GAVE as an open offer. A line 2 whose named giver holds no matching line 3 is an unrecorded gift: the giver has not written it. The fold presents both; a mind rules, as `function:ledger` 2 — and a ruling is an act in a box, never an edit to a ledger, because there is none.
3. It declares its coverage: which boxes it read, at which rung, and which lines it could not sum. A silent box is honest absence, not a gap to be filled.

A page can do all three with no LLM. If a mind writes a reading, keep='collective' lands it at the bare name at the address, where anyone may supersede it with a better one; by default the fold is recomputed on demand and never stale.

## 7 — The clock

A box has no period field, because depth is period. The month stands at the fourth rung — October 2026 is `2026410000` — with weeks beneath for finer grain and the quarter and year above, read by the ladder. The year's coverage is the sum of its months, so carry-over is a read at the rung above and never a stored balance. Nothing says "monthly"; the position does.

"This month" is computed from the clock; no one is asked for an address. An entry appends beneath the month's position by the beach's own verb — `bsp(agent_id='<handle>', block='moneybox', spindle='2026410000.3', append=true, content=…, secret=…)` lands it at the next free slot beneath Forwarded — or from the page. A read at the month — `pscale_stream_engage(field='moneybox', handle, at='this month')` — lays every box side by side at the computed address, and the fold walks the four positions beneath.

The entry's third child records when it was written; its month is where it stands. That is the ecosquared reading of time: the box is a period of intent, not a log of moments.

## 8 — What it is not

Not a statement. A bank statement is points of exchange in arrival order, the traditional shape of the same data; here the month is the unit, and the timestamps are provenance, not structure.

Not a pool. Nothing is held at the bare name. The collective is a reading across boxes, and the house ledgers of 18 October taught the same at the scale of a house: leakage over total, with the rest re-covering the hands.

Not SQ, thanks or credits. Tickets at the fiat boundary (`payway`, `ways:tickets` 7), the out-ledger and receipt (`sand-v2` 3 and 4), and SQ computed from others' receipts (`sand-v2` 7) keep their own law. But lines 2 and 3 are the pound-side shadow of GAVE and receipt — one act seen from two ends — and a forward's named recipient is the raw vote, cast in pounds. When SAND runs, a forward entry may carry its probe\_id at child 4 and the two records become one; SQ then re-reads this record rather than needing another.

Not a bank. A box holds amounts, handles and sentences, never account numbers. It is a public read on an open commons, so the holder voices what they want seen, at the grain they choose — one sum for costs, if they like. A gray line is legal, and the fold reports it as unreadable rather than summing it.
