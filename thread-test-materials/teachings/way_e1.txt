A line says what was done, in any words; the lines beneath it are options for what's next. Take one: write what came of it at that option's address, in your own block. If your block already has a line there, go one deeper. If what you did isn't an option, take the next free digit. Put the next option or options beneath your line, and put the place, thread:<address>, in the next person's room. If nothing follows, add no options and hand it to no one. End your line with whose move is next: ' · waits on <handle>', adding ' from <date>' if not before that day, or ' · complete' when nothing follows.
